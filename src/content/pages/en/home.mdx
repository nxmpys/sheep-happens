## Pick your tree

All three Mage trees deal ranged damage. In Forever each one gets a sharper identity and talents that connect with the other two.

<SpecCards />

## Forever highlights

- **Frostfire Bolt** at level 40: counts as Fire and Frost at the same time. <PageLink to="changes" hash="change-frostfire-bolt">See the change</PageLink>.
- **Ice Lance** and **Fingers of Frost** turn Frost into a freeze-and-shatter tree. <PageLink to="changes" query="tree=frost&kind=new">New in Frost</PageLink>.
- **Heating Up** speeds up Pyroblast after chained crits. <PageLink to="talents-fire" hash="change-heating-up">See it in Fire</PageLink>.
- **Arcane Blast** and **Missile Barrage** are Arcane's new 11- and 16-point milestones. <PageLink to="talents-arcane">See Arcane</PageLink>.
- Less mass farming: **Cone of Cold** lasts less and **Improved Blizzard** slows less. <PageLink to="changes" query="kind=adjusted">See every adjustment</PageLink>.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does WoW Forever launch?

Launch is planned for **4 November 2026** at 3:00 PM PST (23:00 UTC). The beta ends on 21–22 October and is capped at level 30. <Status status="reported" />

### Which races can be Mages?

Human, Gnome, Skyborne, Undead, Troll and, for the first time, **Orc**. <Status status="reported" />

### Which new spells does the Mage get?

Frostfire Bolt as a baseline spell at level 40 and, as talents, Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost, Heating Up, Arcane Blast, Missile Barrage and Arcane Geometry. <PageLink to="changes" query="kind=new">See every new one</PageLink>. <Status status="verified" />

### What is the best race for a Mage?

It depends on what you play: Gnome and Undead stand out in PvP, Troll, Gnome and Human in dungeons and raids, and Skyborne and Undead while leveling. <PageLink to="races">See the races and their racials</PageLink>. <Status status="analysis" />

### Is there dual specialization?

Yes, at level 40 for now; Blizzard has said it is considering an earlier level. <PageLink to="talents">How talents work</PageLink>. <Status status="verified" />

### Which tree is best for leveling?

Nobody can tell yet with data: the beta stops at level 30 and the new talents have no published numbers. <PageLink to="leveling">Leveling</PageLink> explains what already changes and what we will compare after launch. <Status status="pending" />
