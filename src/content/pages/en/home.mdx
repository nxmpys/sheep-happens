## Pick your tree

All three Mage trees deal ranged damage. In Forever each one gets a sharper identity and talents that connect with the other two.

## Forever highlights

<Highlights>
  <Highlight school="frostfire" label="Fire + Frost · new spell" to="changes" hash="change-frostfire-bolt" cta="See the change" featured>
    **Frostfire Bolt** at level 40: counts as Fire and Frost at the same time.
  </Highlight>
  <Highlight school="frost" label="Frost · new talents" to="changes" query="tree=frost&kind=new" cta="New in Frost">
    **Ice Lance** and **Fingers of Frost** turn Frost into a freeze-and-shatter tree.
  </Highlight>
  <Highlight school="fire" label="Fire · new talent" to="talents-fire" hash="change-heating-up" cta="See it in Fire">
    **Heating Up** speeds up Pyroblast after chained crits.
  </Highlight>
  <Highlight school="arcane" label="Arcane · new milestones" to="talents-arcane" cta="See Arcane">
    **Arcane Blast** and **Missile Barrage** are Arcane's new 11- and 16-point milestones.
  </Highlight>
  <Highlight school="frost" label="AoE farming · adjustments" to="changes" query="kind=adjusted" cta="See every adjustment">
    Less mass farming: **Cone of Cold** lasts less and **Improved Blizzard** slows less.
  </Highlight>
</Highlights>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem q="When does WoW Forever launch?" status="reported">
    Launch is planned for **4 November 2026** at 3:00 PM PST (23:00 UTC). The beta ends on 21–22 October and is capped at level 30.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Which races can be Mages?" status="reported">
    Human, Gnome, Skyborne, Undead, Troll and, for the first time, **Orc**.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Which new spells does the Mage get?" status="verified">
    Frostfire Bolt as a baseline spell at level 40 and, as talents, Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost, Heating Up, Arcane Blast, Missile Barrage and Arcane Geometry. <PageLink to="changes" query="kind=new">See every new one</PageLink>.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="What is the best race for a Mage?" status="analysis">
    It depends on what you play: Gnome and Undead stand out in PvP, Troll, Gnome and Human in dungeons and raids, and Skyborne and Undead while leveling. <PageLink to="races">See the races and their racials</PageLink>.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Is there dual specialization?" status="verified">
    Yes, at level 40 for now; Blizzard has said it is considering an earlier level. <PageLink to="talents">How talents work</PageLink>.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Which tree is best for leveling?" status="analysis">
    Frost, at least from 1 to 30: it is the safest and the one the Forever guides we checked put forward. The <PageLink to="leveling">leveling guide</PageLink> has three builds (solo, dungeons and AoE) with their point order. It is a BETA: levels 31 to 60 cannot be tested yet.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
