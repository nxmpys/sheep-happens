## Forever highlights

<Highlights>
  <Highlight image="hl-frostfire" school="frostfire" label="Fire + Frost · new spell" to="changes" hash="change-frostfire-bolt" cta="See the change" featured>
    **Frostfire Bolt** at level 40: counts as Fire and Frost at the same time.
  </Highlight>
  <Highlight image="hl-aoe" school="frost" label="AoE farming · adjustments" to="aoe-farming" cta="How to farm now">
    Less mass farming: **Cone of Cold** lasts less and **Improved Blizzard** slows less.
  </Highlight>
  <Highlight image="hl-legacy" school="baseline" icon="skill" label="Legacy · account system" to="legacy" cta="Which perks to pick">
    **Legacy**: you earn points with account challenges and each character spends up to 16 on perks.
  </Highlight>
  <Highlight image="hl-scrolls" school="baseline" icon="scroll" label="Scrolls · Mage only" to="library" hash="scrolls" cta="How they work">
    **Comprehension**: you decipher the scrolls creatures drop and get one-hour buffs.
  </Highlight>
  <Highlight image="hl-dual-spec" school="baseline" icon="layers" label="Talents · dual spec" to="talents" cta="How talents work">
    **Two builds at once** from level 40 for now; Blizzard is considering an earlier level.
  </Highlight>
</Highlights>
