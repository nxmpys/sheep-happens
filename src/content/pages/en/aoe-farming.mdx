AoE farming means gathering several enemies and killing them all at once with area spells. It is one of the Mage's specialties. In Forever it still works, but **differently** from Classic.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Beta information">
  The rotations and spots come from community guides that played the beta (levels 1 to 30) and from my own analysis. Nobody has measured yet whether it beats questing. I will test them myself after launch.
</Callout>

## What changed from Classic

Blizzard deliberately cut Mage mass farming, because it was used for fast gold and experience at little risk: <Status status="verified" />

- **Improved Blizzard** slows by **40%** at most (65% in Classic).
- **Permafrost** extends Chill effects by a percentage, not by flat seconds.
- **Cone of Cold** lasts less. In the beta data, it slows by 40% for 6 s. <Status status="reported" /> Since 15 October, the first 4 targets it hits are slowed by an extra 10% for 3 extra seconds: Blizzard wants it to keep its power against 4 enemies or fewer. <Status status="verified" />
- **Low spell ranks** get less from your spell damage and trigger your talents less often, Arcane Concentration included.
- **Clearcasting** (from Arcane Concentration) no longer rolls for every enemy hit (15 October build).

**What it means in practice.** In Classic you chained Frost Nova, two Blizzards and Cone of Cold, and by the time the enemies reached you Frost Nova was ready again. Now they leave Blizzard sooner, reach you sooner, and there are gaps where they hit you. Big Classic pulls, like the Zul'Farrak one, are no longer viable. <Status status="analysis" />

## Two ways to farm

### Short rotation (from level 14)

The most reliable one, according to the guides that played the beta. It does not need Blizzard, so it works with any tree. <Status status="reported" />

<Priority title="Short rotation · packs of 3 to 7" status="reported" school="baseline">

1. **Pull** a small pack of 3 or 4 enemies (with your wand, standing still 0.5 s before it shoots, or with a spell) and bring them to you so they arrive together.
2. **Shield up**: **Mana Shield**, or Ice Barrier if you have it. You will take hits.
3. **Frost Nova** when they are on top of you, then step away.
4. **Flamestrike** on the frozen pack. With **Shatter**, it has more crit chance against frozen enemies.
5. **Cone of Cold** to slow them when they break free and **Arcane Explosion** to finish them.

</Priority>

You get Arcane Explosion at level 14 and Flamestrike at 16. With Cone of Cold (level 26) the rotation is complete. One guide uses it for packs of up to seven enemies at higher levels. <Status status="reported" />

### With Blizzard (from level 22)

The classic way, now tighter. It needs the Frost AoE build with **Improved Blizzard** 3/3. With the mana you have at these levels, count on **one full Blizzard per pull**. <Status status="reported" />

<Priority title="With Blizzard · Frost" status="reported" school="frost">

1. Gather the enemies with an **Arcane Explosion** so they bunch up.
2. **Frost Nova** and get as far away as you can.
3. Start **Blizzard** in the last second of the Nova, where the enemies have to walk through it.
4. When it ends, **Cone of Cold** to keep them slowed until Frost Nova is back.
5. Second **Nova**, **Flamestrike** on the frozen pack, Cone of Cold and two Arcane Explosions to finish.

</Priority>

## Arcane Concentration

**Arcane Concentration** gives you a chance that your next spell costs no mana. In Classic it triggered "after any damage spell hits a target", so an area spell that hit 7 enemies had 7 chances. <Status status="classic" />

**Not in Forever.** Since the 15 October build, Clearcasting no longer rolls for every target hit: an area spell gets a single chance, as against one enemy. It also no longer triggers out of combat. <Status status="verified" /> It still saves mana, but it is no longer the talent that defines AoE farming. <Status status="analysis" />

**The Classic trick that no longer works the same.** Casting rank 1 Arcane Explosion to trigger Arcane Concentration cheaply and then spending it on a high rank. In Forever, spells of a rank far below your level trigger it less often. <Status status="verified" /> Use high ranks. <Status status="analysis" />

It sits in row 2 of Arcane. The Frost AoE build does not take it; a hybrid or Arcane build does. <Status status="analysis" />

### The Thunder Bluff spiders

The AoE spot seen most in the beta. Take the north elevator down from Thunder Bluff and climb the slope to the northwest: the camp is around (33, 22) in Mulgore and the spiders, at the north end of the plateau, around (33, 11). There are five quests from level 23. Gather three nearby hatchling packs, kill them, drink and repeat. Do not pull Broodmother Valraxx: she is a level 30 elite. <Status status="reported" />

According to a beta video, each pull gave about 1,600 experience at level 29. <Status status="reported" />

## Choosing your enemies

- **Melee enemies only.** Casters interrupt you and shoot you from range: **Polymorph** them or leave them out of the pull. <Status status="reported" />
- **No fears, knockbacks or ranged attacks**, and open ground, with no obstacles that split the pack. <Status status="analysis" />
- **On PvP realms**, contested spots attract Rogues waiting for you to run out of mana. <Status status="analysis" />

## Mana and survival

- **Drink after every pack.** Mana sets the size of the pull. <Status status="analysis" />
- **Keep at hand** your mana gem (level 28), mana potions and, with Engineering, bombs or grenades for big pulls. <Status status="reported" />
- **If Frost Nova is resisted, reset the pull**: do not try to save it. Blink away; **Ice Block** if everything goes wrong. <Status status="reported" />
- **Skip Frostbite** in an AoE build: its random freezes split the pack. <Status status="reported" />
- **Undead:** Touch of the Grave can break Frost Nova, according to one guide. <Status status="reported" /> See <PageLink to="races">Races</PageLink>.
