## How it works

<Tiles>
<Tile title="Gather" num={1}>

Bring **3 to 7 melee enemies** to you so they arrive together. Casters interrupt you and shoot from range: **Polymorph** them or leave them out. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Freeze" num={2}>

**Frost Nova** when they are on top of you, and step back. That is the moment you deal damage for free.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Burn" num={3}>

Area spells on the whole pack until they drop. <Status status="analysis" compact />

</Tile>
</Tiles>

## The rotations

The short one is the most reliable, according to community guides, and it works with any tree. Use the Blizzard one only when you have room to channel it fully: if they reach you first, switch to Arcane Explosion. Pick a step, or press Play. <Status status="reported" />

<AoeArena />

**If one is left standing and you have Ice Lance,** finish it while it is still frozen or with a **Fingers of Frost** charge: it deals much more damage to frozen enemies. <Status status="reported" compact />

**If Frost Nova is resisted, reset the pull:** do not try to save it. **Blink** away, and **Ice Block** if everything goes wrong. <Status status="reported" compact />

## Where to farm

Pick the spot by **your spell ranks**, not by enemy color: what matters is whether your area spells kill them fast. Once the spot turns green, move on. <Status status="reported" />

Up to level 22, AoE the enemies of kill quests: that is what RestedXP's Mage AoE routes for Forever do, in Darkshore, Westfall, Redridge, the Barrens and Stonetalon. <Status status="reported" compact />

<SpotFinder />

The pending spots come from Classic and nobody has tested them in Forever. <Status status="pending" compact />

### The Thunder Bluff spiders

The AoE spot community guides cite most. Take the north elevator down from Thunder Bluff and climb the slope to the northwest: the camp is around (33, 22) in Mulgore and the spiders, at the north end of the plateau, around (33, 11). There are five quests from level 23. Gather three nearby hatchling packs, kill them, drink and repeat. Do not pull Broodmother Valraxx: she is a level 30 elite. <Status status="reported" />

### The world first 60 route

52 hours after launch, three Mages, a Paladin and a Shaman reached level 60 together on a European Normal realm. From level 35 they only AoE farmed, at these spots: <Status status="reported" />

1. **Dreadmurk Shore**, in Dustwallow Marsh: Mirefin murlocs.
2. **Ruins of Zul'Mamwe**, in Stranglethorn Vale: Skullsplitter trolls.
3. **Lost Rigger Cove**, in Tanaris: Southsea pirates.
4. **Dalson's Tears** and **Gahrron's Withering**, in the Western Plaguelands: undead.

They played as a group and switched layers to find fresh spawns: a solo Mage will not keep that pace. <Status status="analysis" compact />

## Before you pull

<Pieces>

- **Find a free camp.** With the launch crowds, an empty second-rate camp beats a famous one shared with other Mages. <Status status="reported" compact />
- **Count on respawns.** Blizzard sped them up for launch, more the lower the zone's level, and will bring the low zones back to usual rates once the rush of new characters settles. On 5 November it fixed some Dustwallow Marsh murlocs respawning too quickly. <Status status="verified" compact />
- **Choose the ground.** No fears, knockbacks or ranged attacks, and open ground with no obstacles that split the pack. <Status status="analysis" compact />
- **Watch the realm.** On PvP realms, contested spots attract Rogues waiting for you to run out of mana. <Status status="analysis" compact />
- **Undead.** Touch of the Grave can break Frost Nova, according to one guide. <Status status="reported" compact /> See <PageLink to="races">Races</PageLink>.

</Pieces>

## If you come from Classic

<details class="disclosure">
<summary>What changed from Classic, and why big pulls are gone</summary>

Blizzard deliberately cut Mage mass farming, because it was used for fast gold and experience at little risk: <Status status="verified" />

- **Improved Blizzard** slows by **40%** at most (65% in Classic).
- **Permafrost** extends Chill effects by a percentage, not by flat seconds.
- **Cone of Cold** lasts less. It slows by 40% for 6 s. <Status status="reported" /> The first 4 targets it hits are slowed by an extra 10% for 3 extra seconds: Blizzard wants it to keep its power against 4 enemies or fewer. <Status status="verified" />
- **Low spell ranks** (downranking) get less from your spell damage. Since 3 November they trigger your talents as often as the top rank, except Blizzard.
- **Clearcasting** (from Arcane Concentration) no longer rolls for every enemy hit (15 October build).

**What it means in practice.** In Classic you chained Frost Nova, two Blizzards and Cone of Cold, and by the time the enemies reached you Frost Nova was ready again. Now they leave Blizzard sooner, reach you sooner, and there are gaps where they hit you. Big Classic pulls, like the Zul'Farrak one, are no longer viable. <Status status="analysis" />

**Arcane Concentration.** In Classic it triggered "after any damage spell hits a target", so an area spell that hit 7 enemies had 7 chances. <Status status="classic" /> In Forever an area spell gets a single chance, as against one enemy, and it no longer triggers out of combat. <Status status="verified" />

**The Classic trick is back.** Casting rank 1 Arcane Explosion to trigger Arcane Concentration cheaply and then spending it on a high rank. Low ranks trigger it as often as the top rank. <Status status="verified" /> Not with Blizzard: a rank learned more than 12 levels below yours triggers it less often. <Status status="verified" /> Cast Blizzard at your highest rank. <Status status="analysis" />

</details>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem q="Can Mages AoE farm in WoW Forever?" status="reported">
    Yes, but with smaller packs than in Classic: 3 to 7 melee enemies, with Frost Nova, Flamestrike, Cone of Cold and Arcane Explosion. Huge Blizzard pulls no longer work.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Is it still the fastest way to level?" status="reported">
    In a group, yes: the world's first level 60s were three Mages, a Paladin and a Shaman who AoE farmed from level 35 and got there in 52 hours. For a solo Mage, nobody has measured it yet.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="From what level can I AoE farm?" status="reported">
    From level 14, with Arcane Explosion and small packs. The Blizzard rotation needs Blizzard (level 20) and Improved Blizzard 3/3 in your talents.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Why does it not work like in Classic?" status="verified">
    Blizzard cut Improved Blizzard's slow from 65% to 40%, shortened Cone of Cold and changed Permafrost, to rein in mass farming for gold and experience.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="What is the best AoE farming spot?" status="reported">
    From 23 to 30, the most popular is the spiders on the plateau northwest of Thunder Bluff (Horde); for the Alliance, the Wetlands gnolls and Duskwood. From 35 to 60, the spots of the world first 60 route: Dreadmurk Shore, Ruins of Zul'Mamwe, Lost Rigger Cove and Dalson's Tears.
  </FaqItem>
