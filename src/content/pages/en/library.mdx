## How it works

1. **Pick up the book.** Taking it from the ground, a table or a shelf puts it in your bag and gives you a quest.
2. **Hand it in to your faction's librarian.** Each title counts once, even if you find it in two places.
3. **Collect the reward** once you have handed in 10, 20 and 25 different books: the librarian offers a quest with a choice of items. The first 10 count towards the 20, and those towards the 25.

Every class can collect books. Mages get something extra: when they hand in the first one they learn **Study**, which gives you a bundle of scrolls to decipher with Comprehension while in a library. It is all in <PageLink to="library" hash="scrolls">Scrolls</PageLink>. <Status status="reported" />

## Where to hand them in

| Faction | Librarian | Where | Coordinates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alliance | Garion Wendell | Stormwind, inside the Mage Tower | 49.0, 86.4 |
| Horde | Owen Thadd | Undercity, Magic Quarter | 74.0, 32.4 |

Both stand next to the Mage portal trainers: you can hand in books every time you go back to train. <Status status="reported" />

## The rewards and which one to pick

### 10 books: a necklace

<ItemCards ids={['scholarly-pendant', 'erudites-amulet']} />

For a Mage, the **Scholarly Pendant**: Spirit helps you regain mana between fights and Agility does nothing for you. You will replace it soon. <Status status="analysis" />

### 20 books: a ring

<ItemCards ids={['philanthropists-ring', 'field-researchers-loop']} />

The **Philanthropist's Ring**, no question: +5 Intellect and +10 spell damage on a ring is one of the best things you can wear until 40. Reaching 20 books before level 30 is doable if you pick them up on your way. <Status status="analysis" />

### 25 books: a bow, a shield or a light

<ItemCards ids={['researchers-night-light', 'truthseekers-bow', 'crest-of-elucidation']} />

Mages cannot use bows or shields, so the only option is the **Researcher's Night Light**, which only adds Fire damage. If you level as Frost and wield the Celestial Stave, this reward gives you nothing: do not go out of your way for it. Since 8 October the bow requires level 40, like the other two. <Status status="verified" />

## The catalogue

Tick each book when you hand it in: the bar tells you how far you are from the next reward. Ticks are saved in this browser only. The coordinates copy the `/way` command for the TomTom addon.

Books are grouped by the level from which the trip stops being dangerous for a Mage, not by the book's level: you can grab them earlier if you are careful.

## A route for the first 10

- **Alliance**: Tower of Azora (Elwynn), Ironforge, the Sentinel Hill inn and Moonbrook (Westfall), Mo'grosh Stronghold (Loch Modan) and, when you travel to Kalimdor, Darkshore, Ratchet, the Sludge Fen and the Wailing Caverns cave. <Status status="analysis" />
- **Horde**: Brill (Tirisfal), the Sepulcher and Amber Mill (Silverpine), the Orgrimmar mural, Ratchet, the Sludge Fen, the Wailing Caverns cave and, when you can, Darkshore. Moonbrook and Mo'grosh are in Alliance territory: leave them for later. <Status status="analysis" />

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem q="Is the library only for Mages?" status="reported">
    No. Any class can hand in books and get the rewards. What is Mage-only is Study and Comprehension.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Does a book found in two places count twice?" status="reported">
    No. It is the same book and the same quest: it counts once, wherever you pick it up.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="How many books are there?" status="reported">
    Wowhead counts around 40. This page lists the 38 I could place; the ones in high-level zones are not checked in game yet. With 25 you get every reward.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Do I need an addon?" status="analysis">
    No, but TomTom helps: copy the coordinates from the catalogue and paste them in chat to get an arrow to the book.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Is deciphering scrolls worth it while leveling?" status="analysis">
    Yes, if you stop to drink anyway: it costs no mana and scrolls give one-hour buffs, such as the rat familiar (+2 Intellect) or Imbue Frost on your staff (up to +8 Frost damage, from level 16).
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Can you sell or trade scrolls?" status="analysis">
    In the client data, untranslated scrolls are not bound, but only a Mage can read them and vendors pay 15 copper. I have not tested it in game yet.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
