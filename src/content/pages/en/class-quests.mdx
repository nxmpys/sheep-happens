## How to fit them into your leveling

- **From 10 to 26, whenever you visit the capital.** The first three happen in your capital and in zones of your level: pick them up when you visit your trainer. <Status status="analysis" />
- **The wand, with the Scarlet Monastery Library.** The book Rituals of Power is inside: if you run the Monastery between levels 33 and 40, have the quest active. The Arathi and Thousand Needles parts are level 38 to 40. <Status status="analysis" />
- **The Celestial Stave, with Uldaman.** The Obsidian Power Source drops in Uldaman: pick up the chain at 35 and finish it when you run that dungeon. The Mana Surges have a time limit and come several at a time: keep Frost Nova and Ice Block ready. <Status status="analysis" />
- **For Frost, Icefury and the staff.** The +9 Frost damage and the +46 spell damage are the two biggest upgrades a Frost Mage finds between levels 30 and 45. <Status status="analysis" />

## What is not known yet

- **The Skyborne** are a new race and do not show up in any version of the level 10 quest. They may get another one or the human one. <Status status="pending" />
- **The level 26 race versions** may be limited to some races, as in Classic: if you are an Orc or a Skyborne, check that your trainer offers the follow-up before you travel. <Status status="pending" />
- **Xylem's rewards**: the Classic ones are not in the Forever data. <Status status="pending" />

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem q="Are class quests needed to learn spells?" status="reported">
    No. You learn spells from the trainer. Mage class quests only give gear, except the level 60 one, which teaches the last rank of Conjure Water.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Which wand should I pick from Tabetha?" status="analysis">
    The one for your tree: Icefury for Frost, Ragefire for Fire and Nether Force for Arcane. All three have almost the same damage per second; what changes is the school of the +9.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Celestial Stave or Celestial Orb?" status="analysis">
    The staff for almost everyone: +46 spell damage against +13 on the orb. The orb is only worth it if you already have a one-hander with more than 30 spell damage.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Can I do the wand at level 30?" status="reported">
    You can pick it up at 30, but the Arathi trolls, the circle and the Monastery Library are level 38 to 40. Solo at 30 it is very slow.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
