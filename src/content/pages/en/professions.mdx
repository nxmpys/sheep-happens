## What each profession gives at the campfire

When you sit by a campfire, the camp objects of your group's professions give a buff. This is what each profession's first object (skill 20) gives, and whether it helps a Mage:

<CampBuffs />

How it works: placing a campfire creates a camp, and nearby players can add objects from their professions: three at the basic campfire, and five or ten with Cooking’s upgrades. Every profession has three objects, and their buffs are variants of class buffs that do not stack with them. <Status status="verified" />

According to community guides, each player can place only one object per campfire and they all share a one-hour cooldown, so a Mage playing alone gets one buff at a time. <Status status="reported" /> That is why camps should not decide your primary professions: the Fish Bowl, from a secondary profession, is already within any Mage’s reach, and in a group everyone brings their own. <Status status="analysis" />

## What changes in Forever professions

- **The same professions**: nine primary and three secondary, with no Jewelcrafting. Mage-only skills such as <PageLink to="changes" hash="change-comprehension">Comprehension</PageLink> and Research are separate and take neither of the two slots. <Status status="reported" />
- **600+ new recipes** across every level range. <Status status="verified" /> Many craft bind-on-pickup gear and cost **Merchant’s Favor**, earned by filling supply crates and turning them in to the Azeroth Commerce Authority (Alliance) or Durotar Supply and Logistics (Horde). <Status status="reported" />
- **Healing potions**: the regular ones move to First Aid. Alchemy keeps only Discolored potions, which deal part of the healing back to you. <Status status="reported" />
- **Food with experience**: cooked food gives stats and a little extra experience. <Status status="verified" /> +5% experience from kills for 15 minutes. <Status status="reported" />
- **Legacy**: according to the community, each crafting profession grants a Legacy point at 150, 225 and 300 skill; gathering ones do not. The Professions Legacy tree has perks such as Bountiful Harvest (more materials when gathering). <Status status="reported" />

## Conclusion: what to pick for your goal

- **PvE**: Tailoring and Enchanting for leveling and dungeons; at 60, if you will raid every week, swap Enchanting for Alchemy. This would change if Forever raids soon bring gear better than Robe of the Archmage, because then Tailoring matters less too.
- **PvP**: Engineering and Tailoring. This would change if EZ-Thro grenades become cheap and easy to get, since they narrow the engineer’s edge, or if Blizzard restricts gadgets in PvP.
- **Leveling**: Tailoring and Enchanting. If you cannot gather Merchant’s Favor for the recipes in time, Tailoring and Skinning give you almost as much plus some gold.
- **Gold**: Herbalism and Mining while leveling. If consumables sell well at 60, swap Mining for Alchemy.
- **If you do not know yet which content you will enjoy**: Tailoring and Herbalism. Once you know, swap Herbalism for Enchanting (PvE), Engineering (PvP) or Alchemy (raids): dropping a gathering profession costs little.
