## How to use it

- **Level and Talented**: pick your level to see how many points you have (one per level from 10). Each rank of the Legacy perk **Talented** moves one point earlier, up to 5 more below level 60.
- **Requirements**: as in the game, each row needs 5 more points in that tree, and some talents need another one first (shown by an arrow, which turns gold once met). Talents you cannot take yet are grey; available ones show their rank in green, and maxed ones in gold.
- **On a phone**: swipe sideways between the three trees; the buttons above jump to each one.
- **Sharing**: the page address stores the build. «Copy link» copies it as is, and «Open in Wowhead» opens the same build in its calculator.
- **Start from a build**: load any of the <PageLink to="builds">guide's builds</PageLink> and change it as you like.

## Where the data comes from

Each talent's position, ranks and requirements live in my database and are checked at every review. The text of each rank is the game client's, as Wowhead publishes it, in English and Spanish; if the game has no Spanish text yet, it is shown in English. What each talent means for how you play is explained in <PageLink to="talents">Talents</PageLink> and on each tree's page.
