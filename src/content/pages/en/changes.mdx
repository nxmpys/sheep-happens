## How to use the explorer

- **Tree:** “Baseline” covers changes for every Mage (spells, armors and systems). Arcane, Fire and Frost are the talents of each tree.
- **Type:** *New*, *Redesigned* (does something different), *Renamed*, *Moved* (changes row), *Adjusted* (same effect, different values or cost), *Prerequisite* (changes what you need first) and *Now baseline* (no longer a talent).
- **Text:** search by name or by concept (*crit*, *mana*, *slow*…).

Cards marked **Updated** changed during a weekly review; the link takes you to the changelog entry that explains it.

<Callout variant="info" title="Where the numbers come from">
  Blizzard's notes say what changes, but almost never give values (damage, cost, chance). The numbers you see come from the client data and carry the “Community” label.
</Callout>
