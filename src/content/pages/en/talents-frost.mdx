## Identity

Frost **keeps its strength in control**: according to Blizzard, it offers combat control no other class matches, and it gains new interactions that connect it with the rest of the class. <Status status="verified" />

## How the pieces fit

- **Freeze to shatter.** Ice Lance deals 300% more against Frozen targets. Frost Nova freezes; Fingers of Frost makes your spells treat the target as Frozen. <Status status="verified" />
- **Frostbolt is the engine.** Fingers of Frost triggers from your Chill effects, and Frostbolt slows. Casting Frostbolt builds the charges you then spend on Ice Lance. <Status status="analysis" />
- **Careful with downranking.** Blizzard's own example is Frost: Rank 1 Frostbolt at level 60 has a 0% chance to trigger Frostbite. Low ranks slow cheaply, but they do not trigger your talents. <Status status="verified" />
- **Winter's Chill, just for you.** It now only benefits the Mage who applies it, only for Frostbolt and Ice Lance, and it can no longer be resisted separately. <Status status="verified" />
- **Earlier defences, pricier Cold Snap.** Ice Block is now the 16-point milestone; Cold Snap needs 21 points and its cooldown rises to 10 minutes. <Status status="verified" />
- **Easier Shatter.** It no longer requires Improved Frost Nova, which frees points. <Status status="verified" />
