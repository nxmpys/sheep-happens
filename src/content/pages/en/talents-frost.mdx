## How the pieces fit

<Pieces>

- **Freeze to shatter.** Ice Lance deals 200% more against Frozen targets (300% until 15 October) and, since 28 October, has an 8 s cooldown. Frost Nova freezes; Fingers of Frost makes your spells treat the target as Frozen. <Status status="verified" />
- **Frostbolt is the engine.** Fingers of Frost triggers from your Chill effects, and Frostbolt slows. Casting Frostbolt builds the charges you then spend on Ice Lance. Since 15 October, Frostbite and Fingers of Frost also trigger from the Chill of Frost Armor and Ice Armor when you are hit in melee. <Status status="analysis" />
- **Careful with downranking.** Blizzard's own example is Frost: Rank 1 Frostbolt at level 60 has a 0% chance to trigger Frostbite. Frost Blast, the level 24 spell that replaced it, was removed on 28 October. <Status status="verified" />
- **Winter's Chill sets up Cone of Cold.** Winter's Chill is redesigned: it no longer grants crit. Frostbolt and Fingers of Frost-empowered Ice Lance apply up to 5 stacks, and your next Cone of Cold consumes them to deal more damage. It only benefits the Mage who applies it. <Status status="verified" /> Cone of Cold also slows the first 4 targets it hits by an extra 10% for 3 extra seconds. <Status status="verified" />
- **Earlier defences, pricier Cold Snap.** Ice Block is now the 16-point milestone; Cold Snap needs 21 points and its cooldown rises to 10 minutes. <Status status="verified" />
- **Easier Shatter.** It no longer requires Improved Frost Nova, which frees points. <Status status="verified" />

</Pieces>
