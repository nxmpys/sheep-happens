## What sets it apart

What the Mage has that the other classes do not. <Status status="classic" />

<HScroll label="What the Mage offers">
<HCard image="q-combat" title="Polymorph" heading="You turn enemies into sheep">

From level 8, **Polymorph** takes one enemy out of the fight until something hits it. Blizzard calls it one of the best crowd control tools in the game. <Status status="verified" compact />

</HCard>
<HCard image="hl-aoe" title="Area damage" heading="You hit whole packs from afar">

**Arcane Explosion**, **Flamestrike**, **Blizzard** and **Cone of Cold** damage everything in an area. According to Blizzard, the Mage masters ranged area damage, and it is also a way to farm: <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming</PageLink>. <Status status="verified" compact />

</HCard>
<HCard image="q-changes" title="Teleports and portals" heading="You are the group's transport">

From level 20 you teleport yourself to the capital cities, and from level 40 you open portals your whole group can step through. No other class does this.

</HCard>
<HCard image="d-7" title="Food and water" heading="You never pay for food or water">

You conjure water from level 4 and food from level 6, for yourself and for your group.

</HCard>
<HCard image="tree-frost" title="Escapes" heading="You get out of bad spots">

**Frost Nova** roots everything next to you and **Blink** moves you 20 yards in an instant, even out of a stun. Your defense is not being where the enemy is.

</HCard>
<HCard image="hl-frostfire" title="Mixed schools" heading="You can mix three schools of magic">

**Frostfire Bolt**, at level 40, counts as Fire and Frost at once: it uses your higher bonus of the two and the target's lower resistance. Several talents boost spells from other trees, and Forever wants more room for builds that mix them. <Status status="verified" compact />

</HCard>
<HCard image="hl-scrolls" title="Scrolls" heading="You read what others cannot">

Comprehension is a minor tradeskill only Mages have: you decipher the scrolls creatures drop and get one-hour buffs. <PageLink to="library" hash="scrolls">How it works</PageLink>. <Status status="reported" compact />

</HCard>
</HScroll>

**What it asks of you in return:**

<Tiles min={15}>
<Tile title="You are fragile" icon="inv_chest_cloth_04">

You wear cloth and cannot take many hits. If something reaches you and stays, the fight is going badly.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Mana sets the pace" icon="spell_shadow_manaburn">

Every spell costs mana. When it runs out you stop doing anything until you drink.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Damage is your only role" icon="spell_nature_regenerate">

You cannot tank or heal, and you have no pet. In a group your job is damage and control.

</Tile>
</Tiles>

## What each tree adds

All three deal ranged damage. Talents do not replace those spells: they add new ones and change how the old ones connect. You are not stuck with your first choice, because you can change talents at a class trainer.

<Picker label="The three trees" art hover>
<Pick id="arcane" title="Arcane" icon="spell_holy_magicalsentry" school="arcane" image="tree-arcane" heading="Build up power, then spend it">

Every **Arcane Blast** gives you Spell Damage for 18 s, up to 4 times, and costs more than the last. You stack it while your mana allows, then spend that window on your strong spells. It can also trigger **Missile Barrage**: fast, free missiles.

## How to play

Whatever the tree, these ideas come back in almost every fight. The exact rotation depends on your talents and is on the <PageLink to="builds">builds</PageLink>.

<Stack>
<StackCard n={1} title="Get ready before you leave">

Keep your armor and **Arcane Intellect** up at all times, and conjure water and food before leaving town. Visit your trainer every two levels: your damage depends on new ranks.

<Chain loose items={[{ spell: 'Frost Armor', name: 'Frost Armor', note: 'level 1, slows whoever hits you' }, { spell: 'Arcane Intellect', note: 'level 1, always up' }, { icon: 'inv_drink_06', name: 'Conjure Water', note: 'level 4' }, { icon: 'inv_misc_food_15', name: 'Conjure Food', note: 'level 6' }]} />

</StackCard>
<StackCard n={2} title="Hit before it arrives">

Your defense is not being where the enemy is, so start casting as soon as it is in range. Finish with your wand: it costs no mana and lets yours recover.

<Chain loose items={[{ spell: 'Frostbolt', note: 'level 4, slows' }, { spell: 'Fireball', note: 'level 1, 35 yard range' }, { spell: 'Fire Blast', note: 'level 6, to finish' }, { icon: 'inv_wand_05', name: 'Wand', note: 'finishes with no mana' }]} />

</StackCard>
<StackCard n={3} title="If it reaches you, get out">

Your ways out. They are not a sequence: use whichever the moment asks for. <Status status="analysis" />

<Chain loose items={[{ spell: 'Frost Nova', note: 'roots it in place' }, { spell: 'Blink', note: 'puts distance' }, { spell: 'Polymorph', note: 'takes a second enemy out' }, { spell: 'Ice Block', note: 'last resort, needs 16 Frost points' }, { spell: 'Cold Snap', note: 'resets Frost cooldowns, 10 min, needs 21 points' }]} />

**Counterspell** stops a dangerous cast, and with 1 point in **Improved Counterspell** it always silences; **Mana Shield** absorbs damage with your mana. <Status status="verified" compact /> Several of these moments hang on a proc that is easy to miss: my <PageLink to="addons">MageProcs</PageLink> addon shows them in the middle of the screen.

</StackCard>
<StackCard n={4} title="Against several, use your area spells">

<SpellBar spells={[{ spell: 'Arcane Explosion', level: 14 }, { spell: 'Flamestrike', level: 16 }, { spell: 'Blizzard', level: 20 }, { spell: 'Cone of Cold', level: 26 }, { spell: 'Blast Wave' }]} />

Your area spells, the last one a Fire talent. Against a single enemy they are not worth their mana. In Forever **Cone of Cold** lasts less (except on the first 4 targets it hits) and **Improved Blizzard** slows less, so be careful dragging large packs. <Status status="verified" compact /> How to do it step by step, and where: <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming</PageLink>.

</StackCard>
<StackCard n={5} title="Watch your mana">

You recover it by drinking between fights. During a fight you have **Evocation** and mana gems; only effects like **Mage Armor** or **Arcane Meditation** let Spirit keep regenerating it while you cast. <Status status="classic" compact />

<Chain loose items={[{ icon: 'inv_drink_06', name: 'Drink', note: 'between fights' }, { spell: 'Evocation', note: 'level 20, every 8 min' }, { icon: 'inv_misc_gem_emerald_01', name: 'Mana gem', note: 'level 28' }, { spell: 'Mage Armor', note: 'level 34, regenerates while you cast' }, { spell: 'Arcane Meditation', note: 'Arcane talent, regenerates while you cast' }]} />

</StackCard>
</Stack>
