## The essentials in one minute

- **The Mage deals ranged damage** with three trees: Arcane, Fire and Frost. Its strength is never letting the enemy reach it: it slows, freezes and walks away.
- **For levels 1 to 30, go Frost.** It is the safest tree and the one the guides we checked recommend. <PageLink to="builds">See the builds</PageLink>.
- **Talents start at level 10**, one point per level. You do not need to understand them all: copy a build and follow its point order.
- **Already know how to play?** Go straight to the <PageLink to="builds">builds</PageLink>, to <PageLink to="choose">picking race and professions</PageLink> or to <PageLink to="changes">Forever's changes</PageLink>.
