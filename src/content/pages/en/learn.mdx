## The essentials in one minute

- **The Mage deals ranged damage** with three trees: Arcane, Fire and Frost. Its strength is never letting the enemy reach it: it slows, freezes and walks away.
- **Pick the race you like.** It barely changes your numbers and this is the character you will keep. If you want to optimize, <PageLink to="races">Races</PageLink> ranks them by content.
- **Talents start at level 10**, one point per level. You do not need to understand them all: copy a build and follow its point order.
- **Already know how to play?** Go straight to the <PageLink to="builds">builds</PageLink>, to the <PageLink to="professions">professions</PageLink> or to <PageLink to="changes">what Forever changes from Classic</PageLink>.
