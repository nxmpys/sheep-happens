## What sets it apart

What the Mage has that the other classes do not, and what it asks of you in return. <Status status="classic" />

<Picker label="What the Mage offers" hover>
<Pick id="travel" title="Teleports and portals" icon="spell_arcane_portalstormwind" heading="You are the group's transport">

From level 20 you teleport yourself to the capital cities, and from level 40 you open portals your whole group can step through. No other class does this.

</Pick>
<Pick id="water" title="Food and water" spell="Conjure Water" icon="inv_drink_06" heading="You never pay for food or water">

You conjure water from level 4 and food from level 6, for yourself and for your group.

</Pick>
<Pick id="polymorph" title="Polymorph" spell="Polymorph" heading="You turn enemies into sheep">

From level 8, **Polymorph** takes one enemy out of the fight until something hits it. Blizzard calls it one of the best crowd control tools in the game. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Pick>
<Pick id="aoe" title="Area damage" spell="Blizzard" heading="You hit whole packs from afar">

**Arcane Explosion**, **Flamestrike**, **Blizzard** and **Cone of Cold** damage everything in an area. According to Blizzard, the Mage masters ranged area damage, and it is also a way to farm: <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming</PageLink>. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Pick>
<Pick id="escape" title="Escapes" spell="Blink" heading="You get out of bad spots">

**Frost Nova** roots everything next to you and **Blink** moves you 20 yards in an instant, even out of a stun. Your defense is not being where the enemy is.

</Pick>
<Pick id="mixed" title="Mixed schools" spell="Frostfire Bolt" heading="You can mix three schools of magic">

**Frostfire Bolt**, at level 40, counts as Fire and Frost at once: it uses your higher bonus of the two and the target's lower resistance. Several talents boost spells from other trees, and Forever wants more room for builds that mix them. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Pick>
<Pick id="scrolls" title="Scrolls" icon="inv_scroll_03" heading="You read what others cannot">

Comprehension is a minor tradeskill only Mages have: you decipher the scrolls creatures drop and get one-hour buffs. <PageLink to="library" hash="scrolls">How it works</PageLink>. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Pick>
</Picker>

**What it asks of you:**

<Tiles min={15}>
<Tile title="You are fragile" icon="inv_chest_cloth_04">

You wear cloth and cannot take many hits. If something reaches you and stays, the fight is going badly.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Mana sets the pace" icon="spell_shadow_manaburn">

Every spell costs mana, and you recover it by drinking between fights. During a fight you have **Evocation** and mana gems; only effects like **Mage Armor** or **Arcane Meditation** let Spirit keep regenerating it while you cast. <Status status="classic" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Damage is your only role" icon="spell_nature_regenerate">

You cannot tank or heal, and you have no pet. In a group your job is damage and control.

</Tile>
</Tiles>

## What each tree adds

All three deal ranged damage. Talents do not replace those spells: they add new ones and change how the old ones connect. You are not stuck with your first choice, because you can change talents at a class trainer, and the exact rotation is on each <PageLink to="builds">build</PageLink>.

<Picker label="The three trees">
<Pick id="arcane" title="Arcane" icon="spell_holy_magicalsentry" school="arcane" heading="Build up power, then spend it">

Every **Arcane Blast** gives you Spell Damage for 15 s, up to 4 times, and costs more than the last. You stack it while your mana allows, then spend that window on your strong spells. It can also trigger **Missile Barrage**: fast, free missiles.

## If an enemy reaches you

Your ways out. They are not a sequence: use whichever the moment asks for. <Status status="analysis" />

<Chain loose items={[{ spell: 'Frost Nova', note: 'roots it in place' }, { spell: 'Blink', note: 'puts distance' }, { spell: 'Polymorph', note: 'takes a second enemy out' }, { spell: 'Ice Block', note: 'last resort, needs 16 Frost points' }, { spell: 'Cold Snap', note: 'resets Frost cooldowns, 10 min, needs 21 points' }]} />

Two more things that save a fight: **Counterspell** stops a dangerous cast, and with 1 point in **Improved Counterspell** it always silences; **Mana Shield** absorbs damage with your mana. <Status status="verified" compact />

Several of these moments hang on a proc that is easy to miss. My <PageLink to="addons">MageProcs</PageLink> addon shows them in the middle of the screen.
