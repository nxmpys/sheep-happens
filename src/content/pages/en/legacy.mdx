## What Legacy is

Legacy is a new Forever system **shared by every character on your account**:

- **You earn Legacy points by completing challenges**, such as reaching a level with a class or a skill with a crafting profession. Each challenge gives its point once per account. <Status status="reported" />
- **Each character spends up to 16 points** at launch, although the account can earn up to 65. You can spend them differently on each character. <Status status="reported" />
- **There are three trees**: Professions (crafting, gathering and gold), Adventure (leveling and travel) and Resourcefulness (upkeep, reputation and honor). <Status status="reported" />
- **Each tree has three rows**: some perks are available from the first point, others need 5 points spent in that tree and others 10. Some also need a rank in another perk. <Status status="reported" />
- **You can reset them** at the class trainer for gold (10 gold in the beta; nobody knows yet if it goes up on repeat). <Status status="reported" />
- **Cosmetic rewards** for points earned (not spent): a toy rifle at 15, a pet at 25, a tabard at 40 and a mount at 55. <Status status="reported" />

Points earned in Hardcore work in the other modes, but not the other way round. <Status status="reported" />

## How to earn points

| Challenges | What they ask | Points | For a Mage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classes | Reach level 25, 45 and 60 with each class for the first time | 27 (3 per class) | Your Mage gives 3: at 25, at 45 and at 60 |
| Professions | 150, 225 and 300 skill in Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking or Tailoring | 18 (3 per profession) | Tailoring and Enchanting give 6. Gathering professions give none |
| PvP | Ranks 3, 7, 10, 13 and 14; Exalted with the four battleground reputations; weeks 4, 7 and 10 of the Field of Honor | 12 | See the <PageLink to="pvp">PvP</PageLink> guide |
| Adventure | Three dungeon challenges, the Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia raids, exploring Azeroth and the Lord Valthalak questline | 8 | They come naturally at level 60 |

<Status status="reported" />

**Your first Mage starts with 0 points.** The first one comes at level 25 or when a crafting profession reaches 150, so on your first character Legacy matters little while leveling. Spend the first points on Thrill of Adventure and switch to a level 60 setup when you get there. If you will level more characters, then yes: with Talented, your second Mage gets every talent 5 levels sooner. <Status status="analysis" />

## The best setups for a Mage

16 points are not enough for everything: you choose by what your Mage does. Each setup follows the tree's requirements and comes with a buying order, so you know what to take with each new point. <Status status="analysis" />

| --- | --- | --- |
| Leveling | Adventure | Thrill of Adventure and Talented |

| PvP | Resourcefulness | For Great Honor and Diplomat |

**Playing on a PvP realm?** The Resourcefulness setups do not touch Adventure, where High Alert against rogues lives. If they bother you a lot while leveling, drop Field Guide from the leveling setup and raise High Alert to 2/2. <Status status="analysis" />

## Switching setups

Since resetting happens at the class trainer for gold, the usual plan is two setups: the leveling one while you level and a level 60 one when you get there. For a Mage, the natural move is from Adventure to Resourcefulness, because at level 60 Talented does nothing and Thrill of Adventure does not work in dungeons, raids or battlegrounds. <Status status="analysis" />

## What is not known yet

- **The cost of resetting points** after the first time. <Status status="pending" />
- **Whether the 16-point cap goes up** over time, as several guides suggest. <Status status="pending" />
- **Whether Reagent Economy covers Study**, which uses a vendor Light Feather like Slow Fall. By its text, it should. <Status status="pending" />
- **Unfinished perks**: the tree has "not yet implemented" slots and the game data already has others (bank at inns, second home, talent reset…), which could come later. <Status status="pending" />

Some guides say Talented needs Well Rested 5/5. The Wowhead Legacy calculator asks for a single rank, and that is what I follow here. <Status status="reported" />
