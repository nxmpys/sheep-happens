## How talents work in Forever

**Milestone talents** are one-point talents unlocked by spending a number of points in a tree. Classic had them at 11, 21 and 31 points; **Forever adds one at 16 points**. <Status status="verified" />

## Hybrid builds: talents that cross schools

Blizzard wants “more room for cross-school builds”. These official changes make it possible: <Status status="verified" />

| Talent | Tree | What it crosses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incineration | Fire (row 1) | Also raises the crit chance of **Ice Lance** and **Arcane Blast** |
| Improved Fireball | Fire | Also speeds up **Frostfire Bolt** |
| Missile Barrage | Arcane (16-point milestone) | Triggers from **Fireball**, **Frostbolt** and **Frostfire Bolt** |
| Heating Up | Fire (row 4) | Fed by **Frostfire Bolt** crits |
| Frostfire Bolt | Baseline (level 40) | Benefits from most **Fire and Frost** talents at once |

With 51 points and row-1 talents as cheap as Incineration, combinations such as Frost with a dip into Fire, or Arcane with a dip into Fire, look reasonable. <Status status="analysis" /> We do not recommend them yet because nobody has been able to test them above level 30.
