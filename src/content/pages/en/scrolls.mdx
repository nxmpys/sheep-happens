## FAQ

<Faq>
  <FaqItem q="What is Comprehension in WoW Forever?" status="verified">
    A new, Mage-only minor tradeskill. It deciphers the scrolls creatures drop; Blizzard leaves players to discover what they give.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Where is Comprehend Scroll?" status="verified">
    In the General tab of the spellbook.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Is it worth it while leveling?" status="analysis">
    Yes, if you stop to drink anyway: it costs no mana and scrolls give one-hour buffs, such as the rat familiar (+2 Intellect) or Imbue Frost on your staff (up to +8 Frost damage, from level 16).
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Can you sell or trade scrolls?" status="analysis">
    In the beta data, untranslated scrolls are not bound, but only a Mage can read them and vendors pay 15 copper. We have not tested it in game.
  </FaqItem>
