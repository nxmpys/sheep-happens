## Gear before raids

Until 9 December, your gear comes from the level 60 dungeons and from **Pix Xizzix**. Dungeons give random pieces; Pix sells fixed pieces for Reals, and for a Mage they are among the best you can take to your first raid. <Status status="analysis" />

### Pix Xizzix’s Reals

- **Tarnished Undermine Real.** Bosses of level 55 and up dungeons drop it, once a day per boss and character. The last bosses of the level 60 dungeons give 3. <Status status="reported" />
- **Not every boss drops them.** Players report that in The Shaper’s Terrace only the optional bosses do, and in Dire Maul North none of the ogre captains. <Status status="reported" />
- **Cap of 165.** Anything above it is lost, so buy as soon as you can afford a piece. <Status status="reported" />
- **Pix is in Booty Bay** (28.4, 75.8). Her first quest, Show Me the Money!, asks for one Real and opens at level 55. <Status status="verified" />

<ItemCards ids={['arcanoweave-cloak', 'magicians-cord', 'channelers-ring', 'weakness-analyzer']} />

**My order: cloak, cord, ring and trinket.** The cloak and the ring carry +1% hit each, and the cord is epic, Mage only and the piece with the most stats. The trinket goes last: it is the most expensive and its use effect is spent on your first crit. <Status status="analysis" />

If you bought the Blessed Band of Light, Pix swaps it for free for the Channeler’s Ring until raids open: the healer ring lost its hit and the Channeler’s Ring traded Spirit for spell damage. <Status status="verified" />

### The level 60 dungeons

| Dungeon | Why go |
| --- | --- |
| Blackrock Depths | The Arcane Brilliance book and the Blackrock weekly quest (Emperor Thaurissan) |
| Dire Maul (East, West and North) | The Mage water and the Dire Maul weekly quest. The Crescent Key from East opens the other two wings |
| Blackrock Spire | Lower Spire gives the gems for the Seal of Ascension, the key to Upper Spire, which you need for the Drakefire Amulet |
| Scholomance and Stratholme | Plenty of level 60 loot and Reals from almost every boss |
| Blackmaw Hold and The Shaper’s Terrace | Forever’s two new dungeons for the top levels, with gear Classic never had |

<Status status="reported" />

Each dungeon allows **5 entries per hour** since 20 November. <Status status="verified" />

## Gold at level 60

- **At 60, quest experience does not turn into gold.** Instead, since 10 November most quests of level 48 and up pay extra gold. Do not save quests to hand in at 60. <Status status="verified" />
- **Pix’s weekly quest** asks for an item from each Dire Maul wing (Alzzin the Wildshaper, Prince Tortheldrin and King Gordok) or, in the other version, from General Drakkisath, Overlord Wyrmthalak and Emperor Thaurissan in Blackrock. It gives a bag with 10 to 100 gold at random. <Status status="reported" />
- **Dungeons pay more than the weekly.** Players who repeat Scholomance or Dire Maul report hundreds of gold a week just from vendoring what drops. <Status status="reported" />
- **Professions.** Pix sells the Alchemy flask recipes for 50 Reals; the **Flask of Supreme Power** (+150 spell damage for 2 hours) is the one every Mage will want for raids. <Status status="verified" />
- **Mind the bugs.** Blizzard removed the gold and suspended accounts for knowingly farming a Blackmaw Hold group that wrongly dropped elite loot. Those who benefited without seeking it were not punished. <Status status="verified" />

**Journeyman Riding** costs 1000 gold according to Wowhead, with a 10% discount at Honored with your capital. <Status status="reported" />

## The 9 December raids

| Raid | Players | Bosses | Where | Attunement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barrow Deeps | 10 | 8 | Beneath Mount Hyjal; entrances from Hyjal, Winterspring and Blackmaw Hold | No |
| Hyjal Summit | 20 | 13 | Summit of Mount Hyjal, near Nordrassil | No |
| Onyxia’s Lair | 40 | 1 | Dustwallow Marsh | Drakefire Amulet |

<Status status="verified" />

All three reset every week and are level 60. Each one gives a Legacy point when you complete it. <Status status="verified" />

### The Drakefire Amulet

Every player needs their own, in the bags: it does not have to be equipped. Both chains end by killing General Drakkisath in Upper Blackrock Spire, so someone in the group needs the **Seal of Ascension**. Blizzard advises starting early and bringing friends for the elite quests. <Status status="verified" />

### Before your first raid

- **Fire resistance for Onyxia.** Blizzard warns that her fire punishes an unprepared raid. Get gear with resistance and Fire Protection potions; **Fire Ward** soaks part of the damage when you know it is coming. <Status status="analysis" />
- **A flask and elixirs.** The **Flask of Distilled Wisdom** (+2000 mana) is also sold for Reals, as an Alchemy recipe. <Status status="verified" />
- **Your build.** For single target and long fights, the <BuildLink use="raid">raid builds</BuildLink>. <Status status="analysis" />

## PvP rank: the Gadgetzan weekly

**Wizzik Dustfizz**, in Gadgetzan (51.8, 27.2), gives the «Field of Honor» chain at level 60, to both factions. Each quest gives **300 Honor and 500 Rank Points**, and from the fourth on, 750. <Status status="verified" />

| Week | Goal | Rank Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Win 10 duels | 500 |
| 2 | Complete 3 Warsong Gulch battles | 500 |
| 3 | Kill 25 players of the enemy faction in Warsong Gulch | 500 |
| 4 | Complete 3 Arathi Basin battles | 750 |

<Status status="verified" />

- **A new week opens with each reset**, and the ones you missed stay available: if you reach 60 late, you can do them all in a row. <Status status="reported" />
- **Duels do not have to be against someone at 60.** Players clear the first week by challenging low-level characters questing in Tanaris. <Status status="reported" />
- **Three weeks each season also give a Legacy point.** Guides say they are weeks 4, 7 and 10. <Status status="reported" />

How Honor, ranks and gear work, in the <PageLink to="pvp">PvP guide</PageLink>.

## Legacy at level 60

Reaching 60 gives your third class point, and almost everything left is earned at that level:

- **Dungeons.** One point for killing the last boss of every level 46 to 60 dungeon (Master Spelunker). Players report that the lower dungeon challenges do not count when you run them far above their level: do them on an alt. <Status status="reported" />
- **Lord Valthalak’s chain**, in Upper Blackrock Spire. <Status status="reported" />
- **The three raids**, PvP and crafting professions at 300. <Status status="reported" />

Which perks to take and how to switch them, in the <PageLink to="legacy">Legacy</PageLink> guide.
