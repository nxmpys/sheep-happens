## The essentials, level by level

| Level | Piece | What it gives | Where from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | Your race's staff (or orb) | +2 spell damage | <PageLink to="class-quests">Class quest</PageLink> |
| 15 | Robe | +6 Intellect | Class quest |
| 20 books | Philanthropist's Ring | +5 Intellect and +10 spell damage | <PageLink to="library">Library</PageLink> |
| 26 | Second robe (blouse or tunic) | +9 Intellect | Class quest |
| 28 | Darkspear Islands dagger | +36 spell damage | <PageLink to="pvp" hash="darkspear-islands-gear-for-leveling">Darkspear Islands</PageLink>, Revered |
| 30 (level 38 objectives) | Wand of your school | +9 damage of its school | Class quest |
| 35 (level 40 objectives) | Celestial Stave | +46 spell damage | Class quest |

<Status status="reported" />

These pieces cover your weapon, chest, ring and wand for most of your leveling. The rest (head, shoulders, legs…) you will swap for whatever drops in quests and dungeons: look for **spell damage** and **Intellect**, plus some **Spirit** if you run out of mana between fights. <Status status="analysis" />

## From 10 to 26: class quests and the library

The first three class quests happen in your capital and in zones of your level: pick them up when you visit your trainer. <Status status="analysis" />

Meanwhile, keep handing in **library books**. With 20 different books the librarian lets you pick a ring: the **Philanthropist's Ring** is one of the best things you will wear until 40, and reaching 20 before level 30 is doable if you pick them up on your way. <Status status="analysis" />

## From 28 to 35: the dagger, the wand and the staff

If you play **Darkspear Islands**, its quartermaster sells a dagger with **+36 spell damage** from level 28: almost as much as the Celestial Stave, but it leaves your off-hand free. It needs Revered with the Theramore Expeditionary Force (Alliance) or the Darkspear Raiders (Horde). <Status status="analysis" />

<ItemCards ids={['clever-expeditionarys-spellblade', 'darkspear-insurgents-spellblade']} />

From 30 you get **Tabetha's wand** and, from 35, the **Celestial Stave**. Their objectives are level 38 to 40: do them together with the Scarlet Monastery Library and Uldaman. <Status status="analysis" />

<ItemCards ids={['icefury-wand', 'celestial-stave']} />

- **Frost**: the Icefury Wand (+9 Frost damage) and the staff are the two biggest upgrades between levels 30 and 45. <Status status="analysis" />
- **Fire or Arcane**: pick the wand of your school; the staff works for all three trees. <Status status="analysis" />

<PageLink to="professions">Professions</PageLink> craft gear too: **Tailoring and Enchanting** give you bound armor, a staff or orb, a wand and a trinket with spell damage from level 10. <Status status="analysis" />

## PvE gear at level 60

What you will look for at 60 in dungeons and raids, by activity. Almost all of it is pending until launch, but some official changes already tell you which stats will be worth more.

### What already changes for stats

<Status status="verified" />

| Talent or spell | Change in Forever | Stat affected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arcane Focus | Now increases your chance to **hit** with Arcane spells | Hit |
| Elemental Precision | Now increases your chance to **hit** with Fire and Frost | Hit |
| Arcane Mind | Increases **Intellect** (was max mana) and Arcane crit damage | Intellect |
| Mage Armor | More mana regeneration from **Spirit** | Spirit |
| Arcane Meditation | More **Spirit** regeneration while casting | Spirit |
| Frostfire Bolt | Uses your higher Fire or Frost damage bonus | Spell damage by school |

What follows from them: <Status status="analysis" />

- Hit talents still exist, so **spell hit will keep mattering** to avoid misses against higher-level enemies. How much you still need, in the <PageLink to="tools" hash="spell-hit">hit calculator</PageLink>.
- With Arcane Mind raising Intellect, Intellect gains some value for Arcane.
- **Fire damage** or **Frost damage** items are no longer an all-or-nothing bet: Frostfire Bolt uses the better of the two.
