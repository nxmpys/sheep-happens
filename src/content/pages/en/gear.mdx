## What already changes

These official changes affect how you will value stats: <Status status="verified" />

| Talent or spell | Change in Forever | Stat affected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arcane Focus | Now increases your chance to **hit** with Arcane spells | Hit |
| Elemental Precision | Now increases your chance to **hit** with Fire and Frost | Hit |
| Arcane Mind | Increases **Intellect** (was max mana) and Arcane crit damage | Intellect |
| Mage Armor | More mana regeneration from **Spirit** | Spirit |
| Arcane Meditation | More **Spirit** regeneration while casting | Spirit |
| Frostfire Bolt | Uses your higher Fire or Frost damage bonus | Spell damage by school |

What follows from them: <Status status="analysis" />

- Hit talents still exist, so **spell hit will keep mattering** to avoid misses against higher-level enemies.
- With Arcane Mind raising Intellect, Intellect gains some value for Arcane.
- **Fire damage** or **Frost damage** items are no longer an all-or-nothing bet: Frostfire Bolt uses the better of the two.
