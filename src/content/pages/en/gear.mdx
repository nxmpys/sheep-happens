## The essentials, level by level

| Level | Piece | What it gives | Where from | Worth it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | Your race's staff (or orb) | +2 spell damage | <PageLink to="gear" hash="class-quests">Class quest</PageLink> | Yes, if you pass through your capital |
| 15 | Robe | +6 Intellect | Class quest | Yes |
| 20 books | Philanthropist's Ring | +5 Intellect and +10 spell damage | <PageLink to="library">Library</PageLink> | Yes |
| 26 | Second robe (blouse or tunic) | +9 Intellect | Class quest | Yes, unless you have something better |
| 28 | Darkspear Islands dagger | +36 spell damage | <PageLink to="pvp" hash="darkspear-islands-gear-for-leveling">Darkspear Islands</PageLink>, Revered | If you play Darkspear Islands |
| 30 (level 38 objectives) | Wand of your school | +9 damage of its school | Class quest | Yes, together with the Scarlet Monastery Library |
| 35 (level 40 objectives) | Celestial Stave (or orb) | +46 spell damage | Class quest | A must |
| 50 | Archmage Xylem's chain | Not confirmed in Forever | Class quest | Wait for launch |
| 60 | Dire Maul's water | Last rank of Conjure Water | Class quest | A must at level 60 |

<Status status="reported" />

These pieces cover your weapon, chest, ring and wand for most of your leveling. The rest (head, shoulders, legs…) you will swap for whatever drops in quests and dungeons: look for **spell damage** and **Intellect**, plus some **Spirit** if you run out of mana between fights. <Status status="analysis" />

### How to fit them into your leveling

- **From 10 to 26, whenever you visit the capital.** The first three happen in your capital and in zones of your level: pick them up when you visit your trainer. <Status status="analysis" />
- **The wand, with the Scarlet Monastery Library.** The book Rituals of Power is inside: if you run the Monastery between levels 33 and 40, have the quest active. The Arathi and Thousand Needles parts are level 38 to 40. <Status status="analysis" />
- **The Celestial Stave, with Uldaman.** The Obsidian Power Source drops in Uldaman: pick up the chain at 35 and finish it when you run that dungeon. The Mana Surges have a time limit and come several at a time: keep Frost Nova and Ice Block ready. <Status status="analysis" />
- **For Frost, Icefury and the staff.** The +9 Frost damage and the +46 spell damage are the two biggest upgrades a Frost Mage finds between levels 30 and 45. <Status status="analysis" />

### What is not known yet

- **The Skyborne** are a new race and do not show up in any version of the level 10 quest. They may get another one or the human one. <Status status="pending" />
- **The level 26 race versions** may be limited to some races, as in Classic: if you are an Orc or a Skyborne, check that your trainer offers the follow-up before you travel. <Status status="pending" />
- **Xylem's rewards**: the Classic ones are not in the Forever data. <Status status="pending" />

### Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem q="Are class quests needed to learn spells?" status="reported">
    No. You learn spells from the trainer. Mage class quests only give gear, except the level 60 one, which teaches the last rank of Conjure Water.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Which wand should I pick from Tabetha?" status="analysis">
    The one for your tree: Icefury for Frost, Ragefire for Fire and Nether Force for Arcane. All three have almost the same damage per second; what changes is the school of the +9.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem q="Celestial Stave or Celestial Orb?" status="analysis">
    The staff for almost everyone: +46 spell damage against +13 on the orb. The orb is only worth it if you already have a one-hander with more than 30 spell damage.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>

## Outside the quests

While you level, keep handing in **library books**. With 20 different books the librarian lets you pick a ring: the **Philanthropist's Ring** is one of the best things you will wear until 40, and reaching 20 before level 30 is doable if you pick them up on your way. <Status status="analysis" />

<ItemCards ids={['philanthropists-ring']} />

If you play **Darkspear Islands**, its quartermaster sells a dagger with **+36 spell damage** from level 28: almost as much as the Celestial Stave, but it leaves your off-hand free. It needs Revered with the Theramore Expeditionary Force (Alliance) or the Darkspear Raiders (Horde). <Status status="analysis" />

<ItemCards ids={['clever-expeditionarys-spellblade', 'darkspear-insurgents-spellblade']} />

<PageLink to="professions">Professions</PageLink> craft gear too: **Tailoring and Enchanting** give you bound armor, a staff or orb, a wand and a trinket with spell damage from level 10. <Status status="analysis" />

- **Elixirs.** According to Mobalytics, you can have one battle and one guardian elixir at once. The Forever database labels the classic elixirs this way, but the new ones carry no label and I have not tested it yet. <Status status="reported" />

## PvE gear at level 60

Almost all of it is pending until launch, but some official changes already tell you which stats will be worth more. <Status status="verified" />

| Talent or spell | Change in Forever | Stat affected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Precision | Now increases your chance to **hit** with all spells | Hit |
| Arcane Mind | Increases **Intellect** (was max mana) and Arcane crit damage | Intellect |
| Mage Armor | More mana regeneration from **Spirit** | Spirit |
| Arcane Meditation | More **Spirit** regeneration while casting | Spirit |
| Frostfire Bolt | Uses your higher Fire or Frost damage bonus | Spell damage by school |

What follows from them: <Status status="analysis" />

- Hit talents still exist, so **spell hit will keep mattering** to avoid misses against higher-level enemies. How much you still need, in the <PageLink to="tools" hash="spell-hit">hit calculator</PageLink>.
- With Arcane Mind raising Intellect, Intellect gains some value for Arcane.
- **Fire damage** or **Frost damage** items are no longer an all-or-nothing bet: Frostfire Bolt uses the better of the two.
