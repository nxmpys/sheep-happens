## Where the data comes from

- **Spells:** the key spells table of the <PageLink to="leveling">leveling guide</PageLink>, with the levels of Forever's trainer list. <Status status="reported" />
- **Talents:** the point order of each leveling build. If you have the Talented Legacy perk at 5/5, turn on the switch: your points come 5 levels earlier and the extra points appear.
- **Spells up to 30:** the talent order reaches 60, but the key spells table reaches 30 for now. I will complete it with the rest of the leveling guide.
