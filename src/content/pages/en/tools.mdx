## Crit multiplier

Move the **Ice Shards** (Frost) and **Ignite** (Fire) ranks to see how much damage a crit of each spell deals compared with a normal hit.

## How it is calculated

- A spell crit deals **150%** damage (+50%). <Status status="classic" />
- **Ice Shards** raises that bonus by 20% per rank for **Frost** spells: at 5/5 the crit becomes **200%**. All five ranks match the Forever beta client text. <Status status="reported" />
- **Ignite** burns for 8% per rank of the crit's damage over 4 s for **Fire** spells: **40%** at 5/5. It matches the beta too. <Status status="reported" />
- **Frostfire Bolt** counts as Fire **and** Frost, so it gets both: 200% + 40% of 200% = **280%**. That is exactly Blizzard's example. <Status status="verified" />

Other modifiers (crit chance, target resistance) are not included: this compares **how hard a crit hits**, not how many crits you will land.

## Spell hit

A spell that misses does nothing and still costs the mana. Pick whether you attack a creature or a player, its level, your **Precision** ranks and the hit on your gear to see how often you land and how much you still need for the cap. Resistances are not included.

<HitCalculator />

- Against a creature of your level you hit **96%** of the time; at +1 and +2 levels, 95 and 94%. From +3 it drops 11 points per level: **83%** against a raid boss (level 63 for a level 60). <Status status="classic" />
- **Changed in Forever:** in Classic a 1% miss chance always remained (99% cap). In Forever beta players reach **100%**: **4%** hit is enough never to miss a target of your level and **17%** against a raid boss. Blizzard has not confirmed it yet. <Status status="reported" />
- **Against players**, the table is the same up to +2 levels; from +3 it drops 7 points per level instead of 11: **87%** at +3. This is the Classic table; there is no Forever data yet. <Status status="classic" />
- In Forever, **Precision** (Frost, row 1) gives 2% hit per rank, up to 6%, with every spell since the 28 October build, according to the beta client. Arcane Focus is gone. <Status status="reported" />

Some guides give 3% at your level and 16% against bosses: those are Classic's numbers, which keep that 1% miss. I will check it in game at launch.
