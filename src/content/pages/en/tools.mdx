## Crit multiplier

Move the **Ice Shards** (Frost) and **Ignite** (Fire) ranks to see how much damage a crit of each spell deals compared with a normal hit.

## How it is calculated

- A spell crit deals **150%** damage (+50%). <Status status="classic" />
- **Ice Shards** raises that bonus by 20% per rank for **Frost** spells: at 5/5 the crit becomes **200%**. <Status status="classic" />
- **Ignite** burns for 8% per rank of the crit's damage over 4 s for **Fire** spells: **40%** at 5/5. <Status status="classic" />
- **Frostfire Bolt** counts as Fire **and** Frost, so it gets both: 200% + 40% of 200% = **280%**. That is exactly Blizzard's example. <Status status="verified" />

Other modifiers (crit chance, target resistance) are not included: this compares **how hard a crit hits**, not how many crits you will land.
