## Crit multiplier

Move the **Ice Shards** (Frost) and **Ignite** (Fire) ranks to see how much damage a crit of each spell deals compared with a normal hit, and how hard **Ice Lance** hits a Frozen target.

## How it is calculated

<Tiles min={15}>
<Tile title="Base crit: 150%" icon="ability_rogue_eviscerate">

A spell crit deals 50% more damage than a normal hit. <Status status="classic" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Ice Shards" spell="Ice Shards" school="frost">

Raises that bonus by 20% per rank for **Frost** spells: at 5/5 the crit becomes **200%**. All five ranks match the client. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Ignite" spell="Ignite" school="fire">

Burns for 8% per rank of the crit's damage over 4 s for **Fire** spells: **40%** at 5/5. It matches the client too. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Frostfire Bolt" spell="Frostfire Bolt" school="fire">

Counts as Fire **and** Frost, so it gets both: 200% + 40% of 200% = **280%**. Exactly Blizzard's example. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Ice Lance" spell="Ice Lance" school="frost">

300% more against Frozen targets or with **Fingers of Frost**: **400%**. <Status status="verified" compact /> If it also crits, it is multiplied by the Frost crit: with Ice Shards 5/5, **800%**. <Status status="analysis" compact />

</Tile>
</Tiles>

Other modifiers (crit chance, target resistance) are not included: this compares **how hard a crit hits**, not how many crits you will land.

## Spell hit

A spell that misses does nothing and still costs the mana. Pick whether you attack a creature or a player, its level, your **Precision** ranks and the hit on your gear to see how often you land and how much you still need for the cap. Resistances are not included.

<HitCalculator />

<Tiles min={15}>
<Tile title="Against creatures" icon="ability_hunter_huntervswild">

At your level you hit **96%** of the time; at +1 and +2 levels, 95 and 94%. From +3 it drops 11 points per level: **83%** against a raid boss (level 63 for a level 60). <Status status="classic" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="The cap reaches 100%" icon="ability_marksmanship">

**Changed in Forever:** in Classic a 1% miss chance always remained. In Forever you can reach **100%**: **4%** hit is enough against your level and **17%** against a raid boss. Blizzard has not confirmed it. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Against players" icon="achievement_bg_winwsg">

The same table up to +2 levels; from +3 it drops 7 points per level instead of 11: **87%** at +3. This is the Classic table; there is no Forever data yet. <Status status="classic" compact />

</Tile>
