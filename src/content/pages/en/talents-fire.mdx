## Identity

Fire **keeps rewarding critical strikes and good timing**: according to Blizzard, it goes for devastating hits and critical damage. Forever gives it a new way to turn crits into fast Pyroblasts. <Status status="verified" />

## How the pieces fit

- **Crits → Pyroblast.** With Heating Up, each non-periodic crit from Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast or Scorch cuts the next Pyroblast's cast time by 25% (up to 3 times). Pyroblast is a 6 s cast in Classic; at 3 stacks it drops to 1.5 s, just as Blizzard states. The buff lasts 20 s. <Status status="verified" />
- **More crit, more Pyroblast.** Anything that raises your crit chance speeds up that loop, so crit should gain value compared with Classic. <Status status="analysis" />
- **Finish and open.** With Wake of Fire, after killing a non-trivial enemy your next Fire Blast has a much higher crit chance for 30 s. Blizzard extended that window because Mages need to restore mana between fights while leveling. <Status status="verified" /> That crit in turn feeds Heating Up. <Status status="analysis" />
- **Ignite is no longer shared.** Each Mage has their own Ignite, which no longer wastes damage on close crits or double dips on damage modifiers. Several Fire Mages in one group stop stepping on each other. <Status status="verified" />
- **Personal effects.** Improved Scorch only benefits the Mage who applies it: do not expect another Mage to keep Fire vulnerability up for you. <Status status="verified" />
