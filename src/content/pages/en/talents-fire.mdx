## Identity

Fire **keeps rewarding critical strikes and good timing**: according to Blizzard, it goes for devastating hits and critical damage. Forever gives it a new way to turn crits into fast Pyroblasts. <Status status="verified" />

## How the pieces fit

- **Crits → Pyroblast.** With Heating Up, each non-periodic crit from Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast or Scorch cuts the next Pyroblast's cast time by 25% (up to 3 times). Pyroblast is a 6 s cast in Classic; at 3 stacks it drops to 1.5 s, just as Blizzard states. The buff lasts 20 s. Since 15 October every Fire Blast grants an extra stack (a Fire Blast crit grants 2) and stacks are kept if you cast Pyroblast with Presence of Mind. <Status status="verified" />
- **More crit, more Pyroblast.** Anything that raises your crit chance speeds up that loop, so crit gains value compared with Classic. Critical Mass now takes 5 points, for 10% crit in total. <Status status="verified" />
- **Several fires at once.** Fire Power is redesigned with 3 points: your Fire spells deal 1/2/3% more for each of your Fire effects on the target (Ignite, Improved Scorch, Fireball, Pyroblast and Impact), up to 12% more. <Status status="verified" />
- **Finish from afar.** Wake of Fire is Improved Fire Blast again: still in row 1 and still cutting Fire Blast's cooldown, but with no crit bonus after kills; instead it increases its range by 8/15 yards. <Status status="verified" />
- **Ignite is no longer shared.** Each Mage has their own Ignite, which no longer wastes damage on close crits or double dips on damage modifiers. Several Fire Mages in one group stop stepping on each other. <Status status="verified" />
- **Personal effects.** Improved Scorch only benefits the Mage who applies it: do not expect another Mage to keep Fire vulnerability up for you. <Status status="verified" />
