## How the pieces fit

<Pieces>

- <SpellIcons spells={['Heating Up', 'Pyroblast', 'Fireball', 'Fire Blast', 'Scorch']} /> **Crits → Pyroblast.** With Heating Up, each non-periodic crit from Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast or Scorch cuts the next Pyroblast's cast time by 25% (up to 3 times). Pyroblast is a 6 s cast in Classic; at 3 stacks it drops to 1.5 s, just as Blizzard states. The buff lasts 20 s. Since 15 October every Fire Blast grants an extra stack (a Fire Blast crit grants 2) and stacks are kept if you cast Pyroblast with Presence of Mind. <Status status="verified" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Incineration', 'Critical Mass']} /> **More crit, more Pyroblast.** Anything that raises your crit chance speeds up that loop, so crit gains value compared with Classic. Incineration takes 5 points (up to 10% crit for Fire Blast and Scorch) and Critical Mass is back to 3, for 6% on every Fire spell. <Status status="verified" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Fire Power', 'Ignite']} /> **Several fires at once.** Fire Power is redesigned with 3 points: your Fire spells deal 1/2/3% more for each of your Fire effects on the target (Ignite, Improved Scorch, Fireball, Pyroblast and Impact), up to 12% more. <Status status="verified" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Improved Fire Blast', 'Fire Blast']} /> **Finish from afar.** Wake of Fire is Improved Fire Blast again: in row 3 since 28 October and still cutting Fire Blast's cooldown, but with no crit bonus after kills; instead it increases its range by 8/15 yards. <Status status="verified" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Ignite']} /> **Ignite is no longer shared.** Each Mage has their own Ignite, which no longer wastes damage on close crits or double dips on damage modifiers. Several Fire Mages in one group stop stepping on each other. <Status status="verified" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Improved Scorch']} /> **Personal effects.** Improved Scorch only benefits the Mage who applies it: do not expect another Mage to keep Fire vulnerability up for you. <Status status="verified" />

</Pieces>
