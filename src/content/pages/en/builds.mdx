<summary>How to read these builds</summary>

<p class="disclosure__title" id="how-to-read-a-build">How to read a build</p>

- **Use**: what it is for (leveling, AoE farming, dungeons, raid or PvP). A build can cover several uses.
- **Recommended or viable**: in each use, one build is the recommended one for its level; the others are viable alternatives. We only publish builds that make sense for the content: if AoE farming is done with Frost, we do not make up an Arcane one just to have another.
- **Point split**: "0 / 0 / 21" means the points in Arcane, Fire and Frost, in that order.
- **Full build**: every card on this page leads to the build's full card, with its talents, point order, how to play it and what to bring. Leveling builds live in the <PageLink to="leveling">leveling guide</PageLink>.
- **Point order**: leveling builds say at which level each point goes. Turn on "I have Talented 5/5" if you have that Legacy perk: levels move earlier and the extra points appear.
- **Legacy**: if the build uses the Talented Legacy perk, a tag says so. Talented gives talent points earlier (up to 5 more before level 60) and is paid for with Legacy points. <Status status="reported" />
- **Talents**: each build shows its tree as in the game's talent window, with the original icons. Gold: talents at max rank; green: talents with some points; grey: talents not used. Hover (or tap) an icon to see what it does. «Key talents» names the ones that define the build, and «Open in the calculator» loads it in our <PageLink to="talent-calc">calculator</PageLink> to change it.
- **Status**: "Analysis" means we reasoned it from the published mechanics but have not tested it in game yet. Once tested, it becomes "Tested". <Status status="analysis" />
- **Made for**: the game version it was made for. If Blizzard changes a talent, we review the build and log it in the <PageLink to="updates">changelog</PageLink>.

<p class="disclosure__title" id="why-we-explain-every-build">Why we explain every build</p>

A list of points is not enough to play well. Every build says what it is for, how to play it step by step, what it does well and what it does badly. When the situation calls for it, like leveling, it also gets an in-depth guide.

We do not publish builds we have copied without understanding them, nor builds based on Retail or Classic numbers: if something is not known yet, we say so.

<p class="disclosure__title" id="the-talent-calculator">The talent calculator</p>

Every build opens in our <PageLink to="talent-calc">talent calculator</PageLink>, where you can see the whole tree, read every rank and change points; the link you copy keeps your version. If you would rather share it in the format the community uses, each build also has a «View on Wowhead» link. The [Wowhead Forever talent calculator](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/talent-calc/mage) is also our reference for each talent's position in the tree. Its tooltips and numbers come from the beta client: they are a hint, not a confirmed fact. <Status status="reported" />

Remember how talents work in Forever: 1 point per level from 10, each row needs 5 more points in its tree, and there are milestone talents at 11, 16, 21 and 31 points. <PageLink to="talents">More about talents</PageLink>.
