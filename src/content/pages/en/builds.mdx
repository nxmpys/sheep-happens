## How to read a build

- **Use**: what it is for (leveling, AoE farming, dungeons, raid, PvP or duels). A build can cover several uses.
- **Point split**: "0 / 0 / 21" means the points in Arcane, Fire and Frost, in that order.
- **Point order**: leveling builds say at which level each point goes, also with the Talented Legacy perk.
- **Legacy**: if the build uses the Talented Legacy perk, a tag says so. Talented gives talent points earlier (up to 5 more before level 60) and is paid for with Legacy points. <Status status="reported" />
- **Calculator view**: expand a build to see each tree as in game. The ★ star marks milestone talents. Hover (or tap) a talent to see its card.
- **Status**: "Analysis" means we reasoned it from the published mechanics but have not tested it in game yet. Once tested, it becomes "Tested in game". <Status status="analysis" />
- **Made for**: the game version it was made for. If Blizzard changes a talent, we review the build and log it in the <PageLink to="updates">changelog</PageLink>.

## Why we explain every build

A list of points is not enough to play well. Every build says what it is for, how to play it step by step, what it does well and what it does badly. When the situation calls for it, like the level 30 duel tournament, it also gets an in-depth guide with matchups, race and consumables.

We do not publish builds we have copied without understanding them, nor builds based on Retail or Classic numbers: if something is not known yet, we say so.

## The talent calculator

Every build opens in the [Wowhead Forever talent calculator](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/talent-calc/mage), where you can change points and save your own version. We also use it for each talent's position in the tree. Its tooltips and numbers come from the beta client: they are a hint, not a confirmed fact. <Status status="reported" />

Remember how talents work in Forever: 1 point per level from 10, each row needs 5 more points in its tree, and there are milestone talents at 11, 16, 21 and 31 points. <PageLink to="talents">More about talents</PageLink>.
