## Rules I follow

1. **Forever is neither Retail nor Classic.** Even when a spell shares its name with another version (Ice Lance, Arcane Blast, Frostfire Bolt…), values from other versions are **not** used here.
2. **Classic data is only used for what Forever has not changed**, always with the “Classic ref.” label.
3. **Third-party calculators and datamined data are not enough on their own.** They can be a hint, but they confirm nothing.
4. **The review date is manual.** It only changes when someone checks the content against the sources, not when a file is edited.
5. **Every update is recorded.** When a fact changes, its card shows the date and the <PageLink to="updates">history</PageLink> explains what changed and why.
6. **If it is unknown, I say so.** Sections without reliable data show a “Pending” notice.

## Sources I check every week

High-priority sources are read automatically (RSS and per-build notes) and produce a weekly report; manual ones are checked by hand.
