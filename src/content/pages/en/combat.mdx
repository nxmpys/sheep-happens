## How to read these priorities

Each list goes top to bottom: **use the first thing whose condition is met**. They are not fixed rotations, because Forever's mechanics depend on what happens in the fight (freezes, crits, charges).

Several conditions hang on a proc, such as Heating Up, Missile Barrage or Fingers of Frost. If you find them hard to spot in time, our <PageLink to="addons">MageProcs</PageLink> addon shows them in the middle of the screen.

  These lists are our reading of the mechanics Blizzard has published. Without numbers or access to levels 31–60 we cannot promise they are optimal. We will mark them “Tested in game” once they are.

## Frost: single target

<Priority title="Frost · single target" status="analysis" school="frost">

1. **Ice Lance** if the target is **Frozen** (after Frost Nova) or you have **Fingers of Frost** charges: 200% more damage.
2. **Frostbolt** as your main spell. It slows (a Chill effect), which can grant Fingers of Frost charges. Use a high rank: low ranks do not trigger your talents.
3. **Frostfire Bolt** (level 40+) if the target strongly resists Frost.
4. Unfrozen **Ice Lance** or **Fire Blast** only while moving: both are instant.
5. **Frost Blast** (level 24+) when you need to slow an approaching enemy without spending a full Frostbolt.

</Priority>

## Fire: single target

<Priority title="Fire · single target" status="analysis" school="fire">

1. **Pyroblast** at **3 Heating Up stacks**: it casts in 1.5 s. The stacks last 20 s.
2. **Fire Blast** when available: each one grants a Heating Up stack, two on a crit. With Improved Fire Blast, also to finish targets from afar.
3. **Fireball** or **Frostfire Bolt** as filler: their crits build Heating Up.
4. **Scorch** when you need a short cast. With Improved Scorch, remember its effect only helps you.

</Priority>

## Arcane: single target

<Priority title="Arcane · single target" status="analysis" school="arcane">

1. **Arcane Missiles** when **Missile Barrage** is active: twice as fast and no mana cost.
2. **Arcane Blast** to build stacks (up to 4) while your mana allows it: each stack grants Spell Damage based on your Intellect for 15 s from the first one, and makes the next one more expensive.
3. **Arcane Wrath** (16-point talent) and other strong spells to **cash in** those 15 s. This is what Blizzard intends with the redesign.
4. **Presence of Mind** and **Arcane Power** as damage cooldowns (Arcane Power now requires Presence of Mind).

</Priority>

## Multiple targets

The Mage's AoE tools are still **Blizzard**, **Arcane Explosion**, **Flamestrike**, **Cone of Cold** and the **Blast Wave** talent. <Status status="classic" />

In Forever, **Cone of Cold lasts less** (except on the first 4 targets it hits) and **Improved Blizzard slows less**. <Status status="verified" /> Be more careful when dragging large packs. <Status status="analysis" /> AoE rotations, spots and mana tips are in the <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming guide</PageLink>.

## Control and survival

<Priority title="If an enemy reaches you" status="analysis" school="frost">

1. **Frost Nova** to root it in place. As Frost, use the opening for **Ice Lance**.
2. **Blink** away.
3. **Polymorph** a second enemy to take it out of the fight.
4. **Ice Block** as a last resort: it is now Frost's 16-point milestone.
5. **Cold Snap** resets your Frost cooldowns, but its cooldown rises to 10 minutes: save it for emergencies.

</Priority>

- **Counterspell** with 1 point in **Improved Counterspell** always silences (previously 50% of the time). <Status status="verified" />
- **Mana Shield** absorbs damage with your mana; **Arcane Shielding** makes it more efficient and improves Mage Armor resistances. <Status status="verified" />
