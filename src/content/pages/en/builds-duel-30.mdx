## The tournament in 30 seconds

The **Forever Open** is a community duel tournament, not a Blizzard event, run by Tips Out, EsfandTV and Xaryu. These are the rules that affect your build: <Status status="reported" />

- **1v1 duels at level 30**, **Horde** characters only, on the PvP ruleset.
- **Qualifiers on 17 October and finals on 18 October.** The top 4 of each class enter a bracket of best-of-3 and best-of-5 series.
- **One build per stage**, submitted 24 hours ahead. You cannot change it during the qualifiers or during the finals.
- **Legacy points are allowed**, so you can use the Talented perk (see below).
- **At most 1 minute between duels** to heal, buff and drink. Cooldowns do not reset.
- **Consumables are allowed**, except those on its ban list (for example Goblin Sapper Charge, Flash Bomb, Magic Dust, Slumber Sand and most protection potions) and buffs that are not from your class.

## Why Frost

At level 30 you have 21 talent points, or 26 with the Talented Legacy perk (explained below). This is what each tree gets with those points: <Status status="analysis" />

- **Frost** reaches **Ice Lance** (11), **Ice Block** (16) and **Cold Snap** and **Fingers of Frost** (21). It combines damage against frozen targets, defense and control.
- **Fire** reaches **Pyroblast** (11), a 6-second cast that is hard to finish against a player, and **Blast Wave** (21). **Heating Up** speeds up Pyroblast, but it needs crits. It has little control.
- **Arcane** reaches **Improved Counterspell**, which always silences in Forever, and **Presence of Mind** (21). It is good against casters, but it has no Ice Block and no freeze combos.

A community ranking from early October agrees: Frost in tier A, Arcane and Fire weak for duels. <Status status="reported" />

## The 5 extra Legacy points

The Legacy system gives no extra class talent points, but its **Talented** perk (Adventure tree, 5 ranks) moves your first talent point from level 10 to level 5. At level 30 that means **26 points instead of 21**. At level 60 nothing changes: the maximum stays at 51. <Status status="reported" />

- **It costs 10 Legacy points:** it needs Well Rested 5/5 before Talented 5/5, both in the Adventure tree. Every beta character has 16 Legacy points to test, and the tournament allows them. <Status status="reported" />
- **Legacy talents are changed at a class trainer in a major city**, like class talents. <Status status="verified" />
- **It is worth it for the tournament:** 5 more points at level 30 is a quarter more of your tree. With them you no longer have to choose between Fingers of Frost and Cold Snap. <Status status="analysis" />

## The build

It uses Talented 5/5, so it spends 26 points.

### Fingers of Frost and Cold Snap

**Cold Snap** resets your Frost spells, but in Forever it takes 10 minutes to come back. <Status status="verified" /> With at most 1 minute between duels, you can only use it once in a best-of-3 or best-of-5 series. **Ice Block** takes 5 minutes (as in Classic), so it will not be up for every duel of a series either. <Status status="classic" />

**Fingers of Frost** is passive: your chill effects can make your next 2 spells treat the target as frozen, in every duel, with no waiting.

With 26 points you take both: Fingers of Frost for every duel and Cold Snap for the one that decides the series. <Status status="analysis" />

### Why these points

- **Improved Frostbolt 5/5**: Frostbolt casts faster (0.5 sec less at 5/5 in Classic). It is your main spell and your slow. <Status status="classic" />
- **Ice Shards 5/5**: greatly increases your Frost crit damage, and you will crit a lot against frozen targets.
- **Frostbite 3/3**: your chill effects can freeze the target, giving you an empowered Ice Lance or time to get away.
- **Improved Frost Nova 2/2**: Frost Nova comes back sooner, and it is your main tool against melee.
- **Ice Lance** (11): instant, and deals 200% more damage to frozen targets (300% until 15 October). <Status status="verified" />
- **Ice Block** (16): Frost's 16-point milestone talent in Forever. <Status status="verified" />
- **Shatter 3/3**: more crit chance against frozen targets. In Forever it no longer requires Improved Frost Nova, which frees points. <Status status="verified" />
- **Cold Snap** and **Fingers of Frost 2/2**: the two row-5 talents, explained above.
- **Permafrost 3/3**: your slows last longer and slow more. More casting time against melee.

**Left out:** Elemental Precision (hit) and Piercing Ice (more damage). If you miss a lot of spells, swap Permafrost for 3 points in Elemental Precision: the build stays valid. If you mostly face casters, Piercing Ice adds more damage than Permafrost. <Status status="analysis" />

## How to play

### Before the duel

- Your class buffs: Arcane Intellect and Ice Armor (learned at level 30). **Mage Armor** comes at level 34, so you do not have it yet. <Status status="classic" />
- Conjure a mana gem (Conjure Mana Agate, level 28) and refill mana and health in the minute between duels.

### Freeze combos

1. Freeze your opponent with **Frost Nova**, or use a Frostbite freeze.
2. Spend **Ice Lance** while they are still frozen. With **Fingers of Frost** you get 2 spells that count as if they were frozen.
3. Damage can break the freeze, so start with your hardest hit. <Status status="classic" />

### Defense and control

- **Against melee:** Frost Nova, walk away and cast **Frostbolt**. Save **Blink** for when they reach you again.
- **Polymorph** to buy time: with your opponent transformed you can drink or use a bandage. Any damage wakes them up.
- **Counterspell** a caster's key spell. Use pillars and corners to break line of sight.
- **Ice Block** when you are about to die or to wait out your opponent's burst.

### Against each class

General advice based on how the classes work in Classic. Forever changed talents for every class, so treat this as a starting point. <Status status="analysis" />

| Opponent | What to know |
| --- | --- |
| Warrior | Lives by reaching you. Frost Nova and Frostbite after each charge, and Blink for their second approach. Undead ignores their fear. |
| Rogue | Under the rules, they always get their opener. Survive the stun (Orc Hardiness helps), Frost Nova as soon as you can act, and Ice Block if you drop low. |
| Warlock | Fear and curses. Counterspell their Fear, Will of the Forsaken (Undead) or Shatter Curse (Orc) to remove their curses. Push while their pet is frozen or far away. |
| Priest | They heal. Save Counterspell for a heal and do not dump all your damage if they can undo it. Their fear is instant: Undead has the edge. |
| Shaman | They can interrupt you with shocks. Mix instant spells (Ice Lance, Fire Blast) and cast Frostbolt once they have spent their interrupt. |
| Mage | Whoever controls first wins. Counterspell and Polymorph decide the duel; save Ice Block for their damage window. |
| Druid | Shapeshifting breaks Polymorph and slows. Freeze them, get away, and punish them when they shift to heal. |
| Hunter | Their pet chases you and breaks your freezes. Control or freeze the pet and fight where you can break line of sight. |
| Paladin | Tanky and heals. Counterspell their heals and keep your distance with Frost Nova and slows. |

## Which Horde race

The tournament only allows the Horde, and three of its races can be Mages: <Status status="analysis" />

| Race | For duels | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Undead | The safest | Will of the Forsaken removes fear from Warlocks, Priests and Warriors. |
| Orc | Good against melee | Hardiness shortens stuns and Savage adds 1% crit. Shatter Curse dispels curses, but no longer grants immunity and only reduces magic damage by 5%. Blood Fury adds little at level 30, because you have little spell power. |
| Troll | Good for pushing damage | Berserking speeds up your spells for 10 sec. Its other racials barely matter in a duel. |

The 15 October build weakened Shatter Curse, raised Blood Fury's cooldown to 3 min and replaced Axe Specialization with Savage; Berserking is unchanged. <Status status="verified" /> More detail in <PageLink to="races">Races</PageLink>.
