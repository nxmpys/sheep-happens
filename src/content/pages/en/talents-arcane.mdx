## How the pieces fit

<Pieces>

- <SpellIcons spells={['Arcane Blast']} /> **Set up and cash in.** Since 15 October, each Arcane Blast is a 1.5 s cast that grants you Spell Damage equal to 12% of your Intellect, stacking up to 4 times for 18 s, even while you cast other spells. Blizzard wants you to use other spells after building stacks. <Status status="reported" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Arcane Wrath']} /> **Arcane Wrath as the big hit.** The new 16-point milestone, Arcane Wrath, is a 3.5 s cast with a 6 s cooldown and Fireball-like damage, plus extra Spell Damage equal to 5% of your maximum mana. It fits inside the Arcane Blast stack window. <Status status="analysis" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Missile Barrage', 'Arcane Missiles']} /> **Missile Barrage as the payoff.** Missile Barrage moves down to row 2 with 2 points. Arcane Blast triggers it most, and according to Blizzard so do Arcane Missiles hits themselves now, although beta forum players say that does not work yet. Chaining Arcane Blast should often give you fast, free missiles. A Fire or Frost build can take it too with 5 points in Arcane. <Status status="analysis" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Arcane Meditation', 'Mage Armor']} /> **Mana is the brake.** Each Arcane Blast costs more than the last. Arcane Meditation (more regeneration while casting) and Mage Armor gain value in this tree. <Status status="analysis" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Improved Channeling']} /> **Interruption protection.** Improved Channeling now also protects Arcane Blast from damage-based interruption. <Status status="verified" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Arcane Missiles']} /> **More reliable missiles.** Since the 24 September build, Arcane Missiles only checks line of sight when the channel starts. <Status status="verified" />
- <SpellIcons spells={['Arcane Geometry']} /> **More range.** Arcane Geometry increases the range of Arcane spells: handy for keeping your distance. <Status status="verified" />

</Pieces>
