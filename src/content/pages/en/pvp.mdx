## The essentials in a minute

| What | What it does | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rank points** | Raise your rank (1 to 14) and unlock rewards | Battlegrounds and PvP; capped every week |
| **Honor** | Currency to buy the gear | Killing players, battlegrounds and PvP quests; stacks up to 25,000 |
| **Marks of Honor** | Part of each piece's price | 3 per win and 1 per loss in each battleground; stack up to 30 |
| **Darkspear Islands reputation** | Its own gear from level 28 | Playing that battleground |

Rank lets you buy; Honor and marks pay. Lots of Honor does not raise your rank, and rank does not give you anything for free. <Status status="reported" />

The rank point cap rises every week up to the 24,750 rank 14 needs, so nobody reaches the top in two weeks by playing longer. The system is seasonal: when a new season starts you go back to rank 1. <Status status="reported" />

What changes in Forever: the Honor cap is 25,000, PvP gear and seals cost more Honor, critical strikes are not reduced in PvP and marks stack up to 30. <Status status="verified" />

## The Mage PvP gear

Mages have **one set per faction**, with no per-tree variants: the same one works for Arcane, Fire and Frost. It is all cloth with Stamina, Intellect and spell damage. <Status status="reported" />

### First, the rare set

The rare pieces (Premier Magus) need **level 55 and no rank**: just Honor and marks. There are eight: hood, mantle, robes, cuffs, gloves, belt, pants and footwraps. Six of them make up the set:

- **2 pieces**: up to +23 spell damage and healing.
- **4 pieces**: Blink cooldown 2 s shorter.
- **6 pieces**: +20 Stamina.

### The rank 14 weapons

Rank 14 and level 60 only. A Mage has two paths: the staff, or the dagger with the off-hand book. <Status status="reported" />

The staff gives more Stamina and Intellect; the dagger and the book give the same spell damage plus 1% crit and, above all, two items instead of one. For PvP, the staff for its Stamina. <Status status="analysis" />

## Darkspear Islands: gear for leveling

**Darkspear Islands** is Forever's new battleground: 15 against 15, with bases that score points and a central flag you carry to one of your bases. Playing it raises your reputation with the Theramore Expeditionary Force (Alliance) or the Darkspear Raiders (Horde), and its quartermaster sells gear from level 28 in level 28, 38, 48 and 58 versions. <Status status="reported" />

For a leveling Mage, the gem is the dagger: **+36 spell damage at level 28**. It is almost as much as the Celestial Stave from the <PageLink to="class-quests">class quest</PageLink>, but it leaves your off-hand free. It needs Revered. <Status status="analysis" />

## How the Mage plays in PvP

There are no PvP builds in the guide yet: I will publish them once I can test them at level 60, where fights are very different. What is clear is what the Mage does and which tools Forever gives it. <Status status="analysis" />

### Your role

The Mage controls and finishes. Your job is to stop the enemy from doing what they want: sheep on healers, freezes on whoever runs at your flag or base, counterspells on casters, and burst damage when the target cannot escape.

- **Polymorph**: the best crowd control in the game. Sheep the healer or the second enemy and fight one on one.
- **Frost Nova** and **Cone of Cold**: they stop melee. Since 15 October, Cone of Cold slows its first four targets more. <Status status="verified" />
- **Blink**: your escape. With the PvP set it comes back 2 s sooner.
- **Counterspell**: interrupts a spell and locks that school for a few seconds. Save it for the big heal or the enemy's crowd control.
- **Ice Block** and **Ice Barrier** (Frost): they save you when you get caught. **Cold Snap** resets your Frost spells for a second Nova and a second Block.

### Which tree

- **Frost** is the safest: **Frostbite**, **Shatter**, **Fingers of Frost** and **Ice Lance** turn every freeze into damage. Note: Ice Lance's bonus against frozen targets went from 300% to 200%, and **Winter's Chill** no longer gives crit. <Status status="verified" />
- **Arcane** has the biggest burst with **Presence of Mind** and **Arcane Power**, but fewer ways to escape. <Status status="analysis" />
- **Fire** hits very hard with **Pyroblast**, **Heating Up** and **Combustion**, and critical strikes are no longer reduced in PvP, which helps it. <Status status="analysis" />

### In each battleground

- **Warsong Gulch**: defend the flag. Slow the enemy carrier and sheep their healer.
- **Arathi Basin** and **Darkspear Islands**: Mages defend bases very well. With Polymorph and Frost Nova you hold off two or three enemies until help arrives.
- **Alterac Valley**: area damage at choke points and Counterspell on healers.
