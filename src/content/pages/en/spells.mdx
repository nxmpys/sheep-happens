## Teleports and portals

| | Alliance | Horde |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Level 20 · teleport** | Stormwind, Ironforge | Orgrimmar, Undercity |
| **Level 30 · teleport** | Darnassus | Thunder Bluff |
| **Level 40 · portal** | Stormwind, Ironforge | Orgrimmar, Undercity |
| **Level 50 · portal** | Darnassus | Thunder Bluff |
| **Level 50 · teleport** | Dalaran | — |

<Status status="reported" />

- **Teleport.** It only takes you: 120 mana, a 10 s cast and a **Rune of Teleportation**. <Status status="reported" compact />
- **Portal.** Your whole group can use it: 850 mana, a 10 s cast, a 1 min cooldown and a **Rune of Portals**. Groups will ask for one when a dungeon ends, and you can sell them in trade chat. <Status status="reported" compact />
- **Runes** are sold by the reagent vendors in the capitals. <Status status="classic" compact />
- **Teleport: Dalaran** is new in Forever. In the client data only Alliance races can learn it and the trainer does not teach it; how you get it is not known yet. <Status status="pending" compact />

## What the trainer does not teach

These spells and ranks are not on the trainer's list. According to the Forever database, they come from books that enemies drop or from quests. <Status status="reported" />

| **Arcane Brilliance** | 56 | Book |

| Fireball, rank 12 | 60 | Book |
| Frostbolt, rank 11 | 60 | Book |
| Frost Ward, rank 5 | 60 | Book |
| Conjure Food, rank 7 | 60 | Book |
| Polymorph (turtle) | 60 | Book |
| Polymorph (pig) | 60 | Quest |
| Conjure Water, rank 7 | 60 | <PageLink to="gear" hash="class-quests">Class quest</PageLink> |

Detect Magic, learned at level 16 in Classic, does not exist in Forever. <Status status="reported" />

## How much gold it costs

According to Blizzard, training every class ability from 1 to 60 costs 206 to 207 gold, and abilities learned at the same level cost the same. It has not published the price of each visit. <Status status="verified" />
