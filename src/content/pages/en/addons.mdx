<AddonLink id="mageprocs" /> is our proc alert addon for the WoW Forever Mage. It puts each proc in the middle of the screen, with its time left and stacks, and works in combat even though Forever hides your buffs from addons. Out of combat it reminds you of Arcane Intellect and conjured food and water.
