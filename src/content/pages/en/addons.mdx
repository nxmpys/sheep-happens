<AddonLink id="mageprocs" /> is my proc alert addon for the WoW Forever Mage. It puts each proc in the middle of the screen, with its real time left and stacks, and works in combat even though Forever hides your buffs from addons. If you switch it on, it also counts your Arcane Blast stacks. It also counts down your Polymorph sheep and can play a sound for each proc. Out of combat it reminds you of Arcane Intellect and conjured food and water, and each character uses a settings profile you can copy to another account.
