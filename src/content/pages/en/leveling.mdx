## Changes that affect leveling

### Less mass farming

In Classic, Frost Mages leveled and farmed by dragging large packs and finishing them with Blizzard and Cone of Cold. Forever deliberately reins that in: <Status status="verified" />

- **Cone of Cold** lasts less.
- **Improved Blizzard** slows by at most 40% (was 65%).
- **Permafrost** extends Chill effects by a percentage rather than fixed seconds, so it no longer over-extends Improved Blizzard.

Blizzard explains the goal: making it harder for people chasing fast gold and experience to pull huge packs with repetitive, low-risk mechanics, which disrupted the economy. <Status status="verified" /> AoE farming does not disappear, but it should be riskier and probably less profitable. <Status status="analysis" />

### Downranking pays off less

Since the 1 October build, ranks far below your level **get less from your spell damage** and have a **lower chance to trigger class abilities and talents**. The official example: Rank 1 Frostbolt at level 60 has a 0% chance to trigger Frostbite. <Status status="verified" />

On 8 October the low ranks of Frostbolt, Fireball and Arcane Missiles were also rebalanced so every new rank is always an upgrade. <Status status="verified" /> Downranking to save mana is still possible, but it loses much of its appeal. <Status status="analysis" />

### Chaining mobs as Fire

**Wake of Fire** greatly raises your next Fire Blast's crit chance for 30 s after every non-trivial kill. Blizzard extended that window with leveling in mind, because Mages need to restore mana between fights. <Status status="verified" />

### Scrolls to decipher

With **Comprehension** you can read scrolls dropped by creatures. They have been rebalanced and can no longer be used while moving; Blizzard has not detailed the rewards. <Status status="verified" />
