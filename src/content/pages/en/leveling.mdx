## Progression by level

I split the road into three stretches by when your way of playing really changes: talents start at level 10, and level 20 is the Mage's big jump (Blink, Blizzard, Evocation and Mana Shield). Spell levels come from Forever's trainer list (Wowhead calculator). <Status status="reported" />

### Levels 1 to 9: before talents

- **Spells.** You start with **Fireball**, Frost Armor and Arcane Intellect. At level 4 comes **Frostbolt**, which slows the enemy. At level 6, **Fire Blast**, an instant finisher, and at level 8, **Polymorph** and **Arcane Missiles**.
- **How you fight.** Frostbolt, another Frostbolt, then finish with Fire Blast or your wand. Keep Frost Armor and Arcane Intellect up at all times.
- **The wand.** Get the Lesser Magic Wand (level 5) as soon as you can, from Enchanting or the Auction House: early on it deals almost as much as your spells and costs no mana. <Status status="reported" /> Since 15 October you must stand still for 0.5 s before shooting. <Status status="verified" />
- **Water and food.** From levels 4 and 6 you conjure your own. The exception is water at level 5: Ice Cold Milk gives 437 mana against 151 from yours, until you learn rank 2 at level 10. <Status status="reported" />
- **With Talented.** If you have the Talented Legacy perk at 5/5, your first talent point comes at level 5.
- **Limits.** Little mana and no way to escape. Fight one enemy at a time and drink when you drop below half.

### Levels 10 to 19: first talents and Frost Nova

- **Frost Nova at level 10.** It freezes nearby enemies for up to 8 s and comes back every 25 s. It is how you get away when an enemy reaches you. <Status status="reported" />
- **Talents.** One point per level from 10. Your <BuildLink use="leveling">leveling build</BuildLink> tells you which ones.
- **More spells.** Arcane Explosion at level 14, your first AoE spell; Flamestrike at level 16; Remove Lesser Curse at level 18. Against a single enemy they are not worth it: they cost a lot of mana.
- **A better wand.** The Greater Magic Wand is level 13. <Status status="reported" />
- **How you fight.** From range; if it reaches you, Frost Nova, a few steps back and keep casting.
- **Limits.** You have one Frost Nova and no Blink yet: do not take on two enemies without Polymorph for the second one.

### Levels 20 to 30: the big jump

- **Level 20.** **Blink**, **Blizzard**, **Evocation**, **Mana Shield**, Fire Ward, rank 4 Frostbolt and your first teleports.
- **From 22 to 30.** Frost Ward and **Scorch** at level 22; **Counterspell** at 24; **Cone of Cold**, rank 2 Frost Nova and rank 5 Frostbolt at 26; the mana gem (Conjure Mana Agate) and rank 2 Blizzard at 28; Ice Armor at 30.
- **How you fight.** On top of Frost Nova you now have Blink to escape and Counterspell against casters. From 22 you can start trying AoE farming.
- **Limits.** Spells cost more and more mana. Visit your trainer every two levels: your damage depends on new ranks.

## How to play

There is no fixed rotation: you decide based on what the enemy does. Each build's rotation is on its page; these priorities work with any of them. <Status status="analysis" />

### Against one enemy

1. **Open from max range.** If you open with Frostbolt, it arrives slowed and you buy time.
2. **While it walks in, keep casting.** Every second it takes to reach you is damage it cannot answer.
3. **If it reaches you:** Frost Nova, walk a few steps away and lead with your biggest hit, because damage can break the freeze. <Status status="reported" />
4. **Finish** with Fire Blast or your wand, so you do not spend mana on the last hit.

### Mana and downtime

- **The five-second rule.** Your mana stops regenerating for 5 s every time you cast. Finishing with your wand lets it recover. <Status status="classic" />
- **Drink between fights.** Conjure water and food before leaving town; from 28, also carry the mana gem for long fights. Why you drink more than in Classic and how much water to carry: <PageLink to="mage-water">Mage water</PageLink>.
- **Buy water before each rank.** For the five levels before you learn a rank of Conjure Water, vendors already sell that water: Melon Juice at level 15 or Sweet Nectar at level 25. Full table in <PageLink to="mage-water">Mage water</PageLink>. <Status status="reported" />
- **Eat cooked food before heading out.** After eating seated for 10 s, you gain 5% more experience from kills for 15 min, plus the dish's stat. Conjured food does not give this. Which dishes, in <PageLink to="gear" hash="consumables-for-leveling">Gear</PageLink>. <Status status="reported" />
- **Evocation** (level 20, 8-minute cooldown) restores mana very quickly. Use it in a fight that drags on or to skip a stop. <Status status="reported" />
- **Do not downrank for damage.** In Forever, ranks far below your level get less from your spell damage and trigger your talents less. Use your highest rank, except when you want the effect and not the damage: <PageLink to="leveling" hash="when-downranking-still-pays">when downranking still pays</PageLink>. <Status status="verified" />

### Control and survival

- **Polymorph** takes a humanoid or beast out of the fight for up to 20 s (rank 1). Any damage wakes it, and only one target at a time. <Status status="reported" />
- **Blink** moves you 20 yards forward and frees you from stuns and roots. Save it for when Frost Nova is down. <Status status="reported" />
- **Mana Shield** absorbs physical damage at 2 mana per point, so it drains your pool fast. Use it when you get caught and need to finish a cast. <Status status="reported" />
- **Frost Armor** and **Ice Armor** slow whoever hits you in melee. You can only wear one armor at a time: from level 34, switch to **Mage Armor** when nobody will hit you, such as in a dungeon, for its mana regeneration. <Status status="reported" />
- **Counterspell** (level 24, every 30 s) stops a caster's heal or big spell and locks them out of that school for 10 s. It generates a lot of threat. <Status status="reported" />
- **Dampen Magic** (level 12) reduces the spell damage you take and your healing too: 15 and 30 points at rank 1, for 10 min. When you level alone nobody heals you, so you lose almost nothing. <Status status="reported" />

### When several enemies show up

- **Avoid it.** Pull one enemy at a time, watch for patrols and avoid fighting in caves when you can: there is no room to back off. <Status status="reported" />
- **Two enemies:** Polymorph the second one and kill the first.
- **Three or more:** Frost Nova, walk away, focus one and Blink if they catch you again. If the fight is lost, keep moving until they stop chasing you.
- **If you want to pull packs on purpose,** follow the <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming</PageLink> guide and do not improvise with ranged enemies.

### When a fight goes wrong

If an extra enemy joins, Frost Nova misses or you run out of mana mid-fight, stop and choose: Polymorph the newcomer, Frost Nova and Blink to gain distance, Evocation or a potion. If none of that is enough, walk away and let the enemies go home.

If you die twice in the same spot, change area or quest. Insisting on the same approach costs more than turning back. <Status status="analysis" />

### In a group

- **Let the tank start** and wait 2 or 3 seconds before your first spell.
- **Blizzard only once the tank holds the pack**, and never on a polymorphed enemy.
- **Do not Frost Nova next to the tank:** frozen enemies attack whoever is closest. Keep it for emergencies. <Status status="reported" />
- **Hand out** Arcane Intellect, water and food before you start.

### Travel, portals and gold

- **Set your Hearthstone where you are leveling.** Teleports take you to two capitals from level 20 and the third from 30 (120 mana, a 10 s cast and a Rune of Teleportation), so you do not need the Hearthstone to get back to town. Use it to return to your zone's inn. <Status status="reported" />
- **Portals.** You learn them at level 40 and 50, depending on the city: 850 mana, 1 min cooldown and a Rune of Portals. Your group will ask for one when a dungeon ends, and you can sell them in trade chat. <Status status="reported" />
- **Conjure as soon as you log in.** Conjured items disappear if you stay logged out for more than 15 min. Use travel time to restock water, food and gems too. Water and food can be sold in capitals. <Status status="reported" />
- **Slow Fall** (level 12) only works on yourself, lasts 30 s and uses a Light Feather. <Status status="reported" />

## Common mistakes

- **Breaking your own Frost Nova.** Damage can free a frozen enemy: lead with your biggest hit, not small ones.
- **Pulling too much.** Frost Nova in the middle of a pack without knowing where you will back off to, or fighting with patrols nearby.
- **Spending mana on AoE against one enemy.** Arcane Explosion and Blizzard are for packs.
- **Downranking for damage, like in Classic.** In Forever it triggers your talents less.
- **Forgetting the trainer and the wand.** Your damage depends on new ranks, and the wand saves mana on every finisher.
- **Starting AoE farming before 22** or on enemies that attack at range.
- **In a group:** Frost Nova next to the tank, Blizzard before the tank holds the pack, or on a polymorphed enemy.

## What Forever changes for leveling

<FactList ids={['talents-start', 'legacy', 'respec', 'dual-spec']} />

### Talented: talent points earlier

The **Talented** Legacy perk (Adventure tree) gives you your first talent point earlier: at level 9 with 1 rank and at level 5 with 5. The maximum stays at 51 points. <Status status="reported" /> If you have it, turn on "I have Talented 5/5" above any point order table in the builds: the level column then says at which level each point arrives and the extra points, only for players who have it, appear at the end. The setting is remembered in every table.

Legacy points come from account challenges, such as taking a class to level 25. Your first character at launch will not have Talented. <Status status="reported" />

### Less mass farming

Blizzard cut the talents that let players pull huge packs of creatures with repetitive, low-risk mechanics, to slow down people chasing fast gold and experience. <Status status="verified" /> AoE farming does not disappear, but it is riskier: see the <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming</PageLink> guide.

### Downranking pays off less

Since the 1 October build, ranks far below your level get less from your spell damage and trigger your abilities and talents less. On 8 October the low ranks of Frostbolt, Fireball and Arcane Missiles were also rebalanced so every new rank is always an upgrade. <Status status="verified" />

### When downranking still pays

The penalty hits damage and what triggers, not the spell's own effect. That is why rank 1 still works when you want that effect cheaply. Costs and effects come from the Forever client and the uses from Mobalytics. I have not tested them yet. <Status status="reported" />

| Spell | Rank 1 | Top rank | What for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Frost Nova** | 55 mana, 8 s | 145 mana, 8 s | Freezing: you only lose damage, and little of it |
| **Cone of Cold** | 210 mana | 555 mana | The same instant area slow |
| **Frostbolt** | 25 mana, 1.5 s; slows 5 s | 290 mana, 3 s; slows 9 s | Slowing so you can back off |
| **Polymorph** | 60 mana, 20 s | 150 mana, 50 s | Control on little mana, though it lasts less |
| **Blizzard**, with **Improved Blizzard** | 320 mana | 1400 mana | Area slow |
| **Fireball** | 30 mana | 395 mana | Pulling from 35 yards, 5 more than Frostbolt |
| **Fire Blast** | 40 mana | 340 mana | Tagging an enemy instantly |

Blizzard frames Frostbolt this way: at level 60, rank 1 has a 0% chance to trigger **Frostbite**, so you choose between the cheap slow and the talent. <Status status="verified" /> Frost Blast, the spell created for that role, was removed on 28 October. <Status status="reported" /> I do not know whether the penalty also reduces the Improved Blizzard slow. <Status status="pending" />

### Scrolls to decipher

With **Comprehension** you can read scrolls dropped by creatures. They have been rebalanced and can no longer be used while moving; Blizzard has not detailed the rewards. <Status status="verified" />

How to read them, what skill each one needs and leveling tips: <PageLink to="scrolls">quick scrolls guide</PageLink>.
