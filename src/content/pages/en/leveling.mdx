Pick a stretch of the road. Spell levels come from Forever's trainer list. <Status status="reported" />

<Pieces>

- **How you fight.** Frostbolt, another Frostbolt, then finish with Fire Blast or your wand. Keep Frost Armor and Arcane Intellect up at all times.
- **Get a wand.** The Lesser Magic Wand (level 5), from Enchanting or the Auction House: early on it deals almost as much as your spells and costs no mana. Since 15 October you must stand still for 0.5 s before shooting. <Status status="reported" compact />
- **Water and food.** From levels 4 and 6 you conjure your own.
- **Your limit.** Little mana and no way to escape. Fight one enemy at a time and drink when you drop below half.

</Pieces>

</Pick>
<Pick id="10-19" title="Levels 10 to 19" spell="Frost Nova" heading="First talents and Frost Nova">

<SpellBar spells={[{ spell: 'Frost Nova', level: 10 }, { spell: 'Arcane Explosion', level: 14 }, { spell: 'Flamestrike', level: 16 }, { spell: 'Remove Lesser Curse', level: 18 }]} />

<Pieces>

- **How you fight.** From range; if it reaches you, **Frost Nova** (up to 8 s, back every 25 s), a few steps back and keep casting. <Status status="reported" compact />
- **Talents.** One point per level. Which ones: <BuildLink use="leveling">leveling builds</BuildLink>.
- **A better wand.** The Greater Magic Wand is level 13. <Status status="reported" compact />
- **Your limit.** One Frost Nova and no Blink yet: do not take on two enemies without Polymorph for the second one. Area spells are not worth their mana against one enemy.

</Pieces>

</Pick>
<Pick id="20-30" title="Levels 20 to 30" spell="Blink" heading="The big jump">

<SpellBar spells={[{ spell: 'Blink', level: 20 }, { spell: 'Blizzard', level: 20 }, { spell: 'Evocation', level: 20 }, { spell: 'Mana Shield', level: 20 }, { spell: 'Scorch', level: 22 }, { spell: 'Counterspell', level: 24 }, { spell: 'Cone of Cold', level: 26 }, { spell: 'Conjure Mana Agate', icon: 'inv_misc_gem_emerald_01', level: 28 }, { spell: 'Ice Armor', level: 30 }]} />

<Pieces>

- **How you fight.** On top of Frost Nova you now have Blink to escape and Counterspell against casters. Your first teleports arrive at 20 too.
- **Try farming.** From 14 you can pull small packs and from 22, with Improved Blizzard 3/3, use the Blizzard rotation: <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming</PageLink>.
- **Your limit.** Spells cost more and more mana. Visit your trainer every two levels: your damage depends on new ranks.

</Pieces>

</Pick>

## Between fights

<Tiles>
<Tile title="Drink" icon="inv_drink_06">

Conjure water and food before leaving town.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Eat cooked food before heading out" icon="inv_misc_food_64">

After eating seated for 10 s, you gain 5% more experience from kills for 15 min, plus the dish's stat. Conjured food does not give this. Which dishes, in <PageLink to="gear" hash="consumables-for-leveling">Gear</PageLink>. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="The five-second rule" icon="inv_misc_pocketwatch_03">

Your mana stops regenerating for 5 s every time you cast. Finishing with your wand lets it recover. <Status status="classic" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Evocation" spell="Evocation">

From level 20, every 8 minutes. Restores mana very quickly: for a fight that drags on, or to skip a stop. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Do not downrank for damage" icon="inv_misc_book_08">

In Forever, ranks far below your level get less from your spell damage and trigger your talents less. Use your highest rank, except when you want the effect and not the damage: <PageLink to="leveling" hash="when-downranking-still-pays">when downranking still pays</PageLink>. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Tile>
</Tiles>

## Spells that are easy to miss

<Tiles>
<Tile title="Mana Shield" spell="Mana Shield">

Absorbs physical damage at 2 mana per point, so it drains your pool fast. Use it when you get caught and need to finish a cast. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Your armor" spell="Mage Armor">

Frost Armor and Ice Armor slow whoever hits you in melee. You can only wear one armor at a time: from level 34, switch to **Mage Armor** when nobody will hit you, such as in a dungeon, for its mana regeneration. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Counterspell" spell="Counterspell">

Level 24, every 30 s. Stops a caster's heal or big spell and locks them out of that school for 10 s. It generates a lot of threat. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Dampen Magic" icon="spell_nature_abolishmagic">

Level 12. Reduces the spell damage you take and your healing too: 15 and 30 points at rank 1, for 10 min. When you level alone nobody heals you, so you lose almost nothing. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
</Tiles>

## Travel, portals and gold

<Tiles>
<Tile title="Set your Hearthstone where you are leveling" icon="inv_misc_rune_01">

Teleports take you to two capitals from level 20 and the third from 30 (120 mana, a 10 s cast and a Rune of Teleportation), so you do not need the Hearthstone to get back to town. Use it to return to your zone's inn. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Portals" icon="spell_arcane_portalstormwind">

You learn them at level 40 and 50, depending on the city: 850 mana, 1 min cooldown and a Rune of Portals. Your group will ask for one when a dungeon ends, and you can sell them in trade chat. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Conjure as soon as you log in" icon="inv_drink_06">

Conjured items disappear if you stay logged out for more than 15 min. Use travel time to restock water, food and gems too. Water and food can be sold in capitals. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Slow Fall" spell="Slow Fall">

Level 12. Only works on yourself, lasts 30 s and uses a Light Feather. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Tile>
</Tiles>

## Common mistakes

<Pieces dont>

- **Breaking your own Frost Nova.** Damage can free a frozen enemy: lead with your biggest hit, not small ones.
- **Pulling too much.** Frost Nova in the middle of a pack without knowing where you will back off to, or fighting with patrols nearby.
- **Farming too early.** Large packs before 22, or enemies that attack at range.
- **In a group.** Frost Nova next to the tank, Blizzard before the tank holds the pack, or on a polymorphed enemy.

</Pieces>

## If you come from Classic

<details class="disclosure">
<summary>What Forever changes for leveling</summary>

<FactList ids={['talents-start', 'legacy', 'respec', 'dual-spec']} />

### Talented: talent points earlier

The **Talented** Legacy perk (Adventure tree) gives you your first talent point earlier: at level 9 with 1 rank and at level 5 with 5. The maximum stays at 51 points. <Status status="reported" /> If you have it, turn on "I have Talented 5/5" above any point order table in the builds: the level column then says at which level each point arrives and the extra points, only for players who have it, appear at the end. The setting is remembered in every table.

Legacy points come from account challenges, such as taking a class to level 25. Your first character at launch will not have Talented. <Status status="reported" />

### Less mass farming

AoE farming does not disappear, but it is riskier: see the <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming</PageLink> guide.

### Downranking pays off less

Since the 1 October build, ranks far below your level get less from your spell damage and trigger your abilities and talents less. On 8 October the low ranks of Frostbolt, Fireball and Arcane Missiles were also rebalanced so every new rank is always an upgrade. <Status status="verified" />

### When downranking still pays

The penalty hits damage and what triggers, not the spell's own effect. That is why rank 1 still works when you want that effect cheaply. Costs and effects come from the Forever client and the uses from Mobalytics. I have not tested them yet. <Status status="reported" />

| Spell | Rank 1 | Top rank | What for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Frost Nova** | 55 mana, 8 s | 145 mana, 8 s | Freezing: you only lose damage, and little of it |
| **Cone of Cold** | 210 mana | 555 mana | The same instant area slow |
| **Frostbolt** | 25 mana, 1.5 s; slows 5 s | 290 mana, 3 s; slows 9 s | Slowing so you can back off |
| **Polymorph** | 60 mana, 20 s | 150 mana, 50 s | Control on little mana, though it lasts less |
| **Blizzard**, with **Improved Blizzard** | 320 mana | 1400 mana | Area slow |
| **Fireball** | 30 mana | 395 mana | Pulling from 35 yards, 5 more than Frostbolt |
| **Fire Blast** | 40 mana | 340 mana | Tagging an enemy instantly |

Blizzard frames Frostbolt this way: at level 60, rank 1 has a 0% chance to trigger **Frostbite**, so you choose between the cheap slow and the talent. <Status status="verified" /> Frost Blast, the spell created for that role, was removed on 28 October. <Status status="reported" /> I do not know whether the penalty also reduces the Improved Blizzard slow. <Status status="pending" />

</details>
