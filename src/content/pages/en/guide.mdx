## What sets it apart

What the Mage has that the other classes do not, and what it asks of you in return. <Status status="classic" />

<Picker label="What the Mage offers" hover>
<Pick id="travel" title="Teleports and portals" icon="spell_arcane_portalstormwind" heading="You are the group's transport">

From level 20 you teleport yourself to the capital cities, and from level 40 you open portals your whole group can step through. No other class does this.

</Pick>
<Pick id="water" title="Food and water" spell="Conjure Water" icon="inv_drink_06" heading="You never pay for food or water">

You conjure water from level 4 and food from level 6, for yourself and for your group. How much to carry and why it matters: <PageLink to="mage-water">Mage water</PageLink>.

</Pick>
<Pick id="polymorph" title="Polymorph" spell="Polymorph" heading="You turn enemies into sheep">

From level 8, **Polymorph** takes one enemy out of the fight until something hits it. Blizzard calls it one of the best crowd control tools in the game. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Pick>
<Pick id="aoe" title="Area damage" spell="Blizzard" heading="You hit whole packs from afar">

**Arcane Explosion**, **Flamestrike**, **Blizzard** and **Cone of Cold** damage everything in an area. According to Blizzard, the Mage masters ranged area damage, and it is also a way to farm: <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming</PageLink>. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Pick>
<Pick id="escape" title="Escapes" spell="Blink" heading="You get out of bad spots">

**Frost Nova** roots everything next to you and **Blink** moves you 20 yards in an instant, even out of a stun. Your defense is not being where the enemy is.

</Pick>
<Pick id="mixed" title="Mixed schools" spell="Frostfire Bolt" heading="You can mix three schools of magic">

**Frostfire Bolt**, at level 40, counts as Fire and Frost at once, and several talents boost spells from other trees. Forever wants more room for builds that mix them. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Pick>
<Pick id="scrolls" title="Scrolls" icon="inv_scroll_03" heading="You read what others cannot">

Comprehension is a minor tradeskill only Mages have: you decipher the scrolls creatures drop and get one-hour buffs. <PageLink to="scrolls">How it works</PageLink>. <Status status="reported" compact />

</Pick>
</Picker>

**What it asks of you:**

<Tiles min={15}>
<Tile title="You are fragile" icon="inv_chest_cloth_04">

You wear cloth and cannot take many hits. If something reaches you and stays, the fight is going badly.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Mana runs out" icon="spell_shadow_manaburn">

Every spell costs mana. When it runs out you stop doing anything until you drink.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Damage is your only role" icon="spell_nature_regenerate">

You cannot tank or heal, and you have no pet. In a group your job is damage and control.

</Tile>
</Tiles>

## Three ways to play it

All three trees deal ranged damage. They differ in how they get there, and you are not stuck with your first choice: you can change talents at a class trainer.

<Picker label="The three trees">
<Pick id="arcane" title="Arcane" icon="spell_holy_magicalsentry" school="arcane" heading="Build up, then cash in">

**Arcane Blast** gets stronger and more expensive with every cast. You spend that power on a big hit before it fades, and you watch your mana closely.

<SpellBar spells={['Arcane Blast', 'Arcane Wrath', 'Missile Barrage']} />

<PageLink to="talents-arcane">Arcane in detail</PageLink>

</Pick>
<Pick id="fire" title="Fire" icon="spell_fire_firebolt02" school="fire" heading="Chase critical strikes">

Your crits charge up **Pyroblast** until a slow, huge spell casts in 1.5 s. Very satisfying when crits flow; at low level you score few, and nothing slows the enemy.

<SpellBar spells={['Pyroblast', 'Heating Up', 'Combustion']} />

<PageLink to="talents-fire">Fire in detail</PageLink>

</Pick>
<Pick id="frost" title="Frost" icon="spell_frost_frostbolt02" school="frost" heading="Freeze it, then shatter it">

You slow and freeze enemies so they never reach you, and hit what is frozen with **Ice Lance**. One main spell, which makes it the easiest tree to learn.

<SpellBar spells={['Frostbolt', 'Ice Lance', 'Fingers of Frost']} />

## Three things to keep in mind

<Tiles>
<Tile title="Mana sets the pace" icon="inv_drink_06">

You recover it by drinking between fights. During a fight you have **Evocation** and mana gems; only effects like **Mage Armor** or **Arcane Meditation** let Spirit keep regenerating it while you cast. <Status status="classic" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Freezing is also damage" spell="Ice Lance">

**Ice Lance** deals 200% more against Frozen targets, so **Frost Nova** can be an opportunity, not just an escape. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Tile>
<Tile title="Some enemies resist a school" spell="Frostfire Bolt">

Every spell belongs to a school. **Frostfire Bolt** uses your higher bonus of Fire and Frost and the target's lower resistance. <Status status="verified" compact />

</Tile>
</Tiles>
