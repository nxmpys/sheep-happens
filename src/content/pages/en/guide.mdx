## What the Mage is in Forever

World of Warcraft: Forever brings back the original Azeroth and expands it with new content at every level range. The Mage keeps its classic role: **ranged damage, crowd control and group utility** (food, water, teleports).

Blizzard sums it up like this: the Mage has one of the best crowd control tools in **Polymorph** and masters ranged area damage, but is extremely fragile if caught in melee. The answer is specialization: Arcane manipulates and consumes mana for more damage, Fire goes for devastating hits and critical damage, and Frost offers combat control no other class matches. <Status status="verified" />

Forever's changes aim to make **each school of magic ask for active decisions moment to moment** and to make **more room for builds that mix schools**. <Status status="verified" />

## Fundamentals

### Mana is your real limit

Every spell costs mana. When you run dry, the fight is over for you. You get it back like this: <Status status="classic" />

- **Out of combat:** drinking the water you conjure yourself (Conjure Water).
- **While casting:** only through effects that let Spirit regeneration continue while casting, such as **Mage Armor** or the **Arcane Meditation** talent.
- **In emergencies:** with Evocation and mana gems.

In Forever, Mage Armor regenerates more mana from Spirit, and Arcane Meditation improves too, although it now requires Arcane Concentration. <Status status="verified" />

### Distance, control and escape

The Mage wears cloth and cannot take many hits. Its defence is **not being where the enemy is**: <Status status="classic" />

1. **Frost Nova** roots nearby enemies in place.
2. **Blink** puts distance between you instantly.
3. **Polymorph** takes one enemy out of the fight (any damage wakes it).
4. **Counterspell** stops a dangerous cast. In Forever, 1 point in **Improved Counterspell** makes the silence certain instead of a 50% chance. <Status status="verified" />

In Forever, freezing an enemy is also a **damage opportunity**: **Ice Lance** deals 200% more against Frozen targets. <Status status="verified" />

### Schools and resistances

Every spell belongs to a school (Arcane, Fire or Frost), and some enemies resist some schools better than others. <Status status="classic" /> The new **Frostfire Bolt** counts as Fire and Frost at once: it uses your **higher bonus** of the two and the target's **lower resistance**, and most talents of both schools affect it. <Status status="verified" />

## What changes compared with Classic

Blizzard has published 40+ Mage changes, and the beta notes add more every week. The most important ones to start with:

- **New spells and talents:** Frostfire Bolt (baseline, level 40), Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost, Heating Up, Arcane Blast, Arcane Wrath, Missile Barrage and Arcane Geometry.
- **A new 16-point milestone talent** in every tree, on top of the 11-, 21- and 31-point ones.
- **Group effects become personal:** Ignite, Improved Scorch and Winter's Chill now only benefit the Mage who applies them.
- **Less mass farming:** Cone of Cold lasts less and Improved Blizzard slows by at most 40% (was 65%). How to farm now, in the <PageLink to="aoe-farming">AoE farming guide</PageLink>.
- **Downranking pays off less:** very low ranks get less from your spell damage and trigger your talents less often.
- **Comprehension:** a Mage-only minor tradeskill to decipher scrolls dropped by creatures. How to use it, in the <PageLink to="scrolls">quick scrolls guide</PageLink>.
