Builds tell you which talents to take; these guides cover the rest: which race to start with, which professions to learn, which class quests are worth the trip and how PvP works in Forever. Each one is written for the Mage, with beta items and numbers.

<HubCards group="guides" />

## Where to start

- **You are creating your Mage.** Pick your <PageLink to="races">race</PageLink> and <PageLink to="professions">professions</PageLink> first: they are the two choices that are hardest to change.
- **You want to make the most of your account.** <PageLink to="legacy">Legacy</PageLink> explains which perks to take with your points for leveling, raids, PvP or gold.
- **You are already leveling.** The <PageLink to="class-quests">class quests</PageLink> give you a robe at 15 and 26, a wand at 30 and the best staff until 50. Pick up <PageLink to="library">library books</PageLink> on the way: the first one teaches you Study, and 20 of them get you a ring with spell damage.
- **You want to make the most of your breaks.** <PageLink to="scrolls">Scrolls</PageLink> are deciphered while you drink and give one-hour buffs.
- **You are into player combat.** The <PageLink to="pvp">PvP</PageLink> guide explains Honor, ranks, the Mage gear and the Darkspear Islands gear you can already use at level 28.

The class basics (trees, talents, combat and gear) are in <PageLink to="learn">Learn</PageLink>, and builds ready to copy in <PageLink to="builds">Builds</PageLink>.
