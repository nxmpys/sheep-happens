## How we rate racials

- **Effect**: what each racial does according to the beta data, as collected by community guides and the Wowhead calculator. That is why they carry the "Community" badge. <Status status="reported" /> The 15 October changes (see below) come from the official beta notes. <Status status="verified" />
- **Value for a Mage**: our rating (High, Medium, Low or None) of how much it helps a Mage, and why. It is analysis, not an official fact. <Status status="analysis" />

## 15 October changes

On the official podcast of 8 October, Blizzard said it would review the weapon racials and the Orc and Troll actives. The 15 October build did it: <Status status="verified" />

- **Human:** Sword Specialization becomes **Ruthless**: 2% more crit, no longer tied to a weapon.
- **Orc:** Axe Specialization becomes **Savage**: 1% more crit. Blood Fury's cooldown rises to 3 min, and Shatter Curse dispels curses instead of granting immunity, with 5% less magic damage taken (was 15%).
- **Gnome:** Eureka! lasts 8 s instead of 3 uses, with the same effect.
- **Skyborne:** Wind Blessed rises to 3% haste, Read Ley Line can be cast while moving and lasts at least 30 s, and Walk on Air drops to a 90 s cooldown. There are more ley lines in most zones.
- **Undead:** Touch of the Grave has a 30% chance for casters and a 25 s internal cooldown: more consistent.
- **Troll:** Regeneration rises to 20%.

Skyborne and Human come out ahead for a Mage; Orc loses PvP defence. <Status status="analysis" />
