## How I rate racials

- **Effect**: what each racial does according to the Forever client data, as collected by community guides and the Wowhead calculator. That is why they carry the "Community" badge. <Status status="reported" /> The changes from Classic (see below) come from Blizzard's official notes. <Status status="verified" />
- **Value for a Mage**: my rating (High, Medium, Low or None) of how much it helps a Mage, and why. It is analysis, not an official fact. <Status status="analysis" />
- **Simulations**: Mobalytics says all racials land within 1% of each other in simulation, without publishing the numbers. I have not been able to check it. <Status status="reported" />

## What changed from Classic

If you know Classic, or you just want to see how they got here: the 15 October build and the launch tuning announced on 26 October, together. <Status status="verified" />

<Tiles min={20}>
<Tile title="Human" icon="race_human_male">

Sword Specialization becomes **Ruthless**: crit no longer tied to a weapon, 2% on 15 October and 1% at launch. The Human Spirit rises to 10% Spirit (was 5%).

</Tile>
<Tile title="Gnome" icon="race_gnome_male">

Eureka! lasts 8 s instead of 3 uses, with the same effect.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Skyborne" icon="race_skyborne_male">

Wind Blessed: 3% haste on 15 October, 2% at launch. Read Ley Line increases regeneration by 50% for 15 s and can be cast while moving; at a ley line it grants Attuned for 1 hour, which raises it to 100% and persists through death. It cannot be used while Innervate is active. Walk on Air drops to a 90 s cooldown, and there are more ley lines in most zones.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Orc" icon="race_orc_male">

Axe Specialization became Savage on 15 October, and Savage is removed for launch: Command returns, which only increases Warlock and Hunter pet damage. Blood Fury's cooldown rose to 3 min on 15 October and goes back to 2 min for launch: the announcement does not mention it, but it is already in the Forever data. <Status status="reported" compact /> Shatter Curse dispels curses instead of granting immunity, with 5% less magic damage taken (was 15%).

</Tile>
<Tile title="Undead" icon="race_scourge_male">

Touch of the Grave has a 30% chance for casters and a 25 s internal cooldown: more consistent.

</Tile>
<Tile title="Troll" icon="race_troll_male">

Regeneration rises to 20%.

</Tile>
</Tiles>

For dungeons and raids Skyborne moves ahead of Human; Human gains mana regeneration, and Orc is left with only Blood Fury for damage and loses PvP defense. <Status status="analysis" />
