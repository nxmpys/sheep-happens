## The short answer

The best race depends on what you will spend most of your time on. This is our recommendation with the beta racials: <Status status="analysis" />

| Situation | Alliance | Horde | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leveling | Skyborne · Gnome | Undead · Troll | Less downtime: Read Ley Line and Cannibalize restore mana, Expansive Mind adds mana and Beast Slaying hits Beasts harder. |
| Dungeons and raids | Gnome · Human | Troll · Orc | Damage bursts (Eureka!, Berserking, Blood Fury) and, with a sword, the crit from Sword Specialization. |
| PvP and duels | Gnome · Human | Undead · Orc | Breaking control: Escape Artist, Will to Survive, Will of the Forsaken, Hardiness and Shatter Curse. |
| AoE farming | Gnome | Undead | Escape Artist breaks roots and slows while you kite packs, and Cannibalize shortens breaks. |

If you are not chasing every last bit of performance, pick the race you like: the gap between races is small compared to playing well.

## How we rate racials

- **Effect**: what each racial does according to the beta data, as collected by community guides and the Wowhead calculator. That is why they carry the "Community" badge. <Status status="reported" />
- **Value for a Mage**: our rating (High, Medium, Low or None) of how much it helps a Mage, and why. It is analysis, not an official fact. <Status status="analysis" />

## Planned changes

On the official podcast of 8 October, Blizzard said the Orc and Troll active racials may be a little too strong, and that it is also deciding whether to keep or replace the weapon racials. Changes could arrive from the week of 13 October. <Status status="reported" />
